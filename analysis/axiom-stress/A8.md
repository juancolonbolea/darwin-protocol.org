## Statement (as in spec)

Investability requires independent, algorithmic standardisation: credibility (P1) attracts confidence (P2) only when expressed as a comparable instrument manufactured by a trusted, independent process. Grounded in P1+P2+P4+A9.

## Derivation attempt

Three claims, one number.

**(a) Commensurability** — allocation needs a shared comparison surface. Derivable, and worse: P2's definition already asserts it ("without comparability, K = 0") and A2 asserts the manufacturing step by citing A8. A8 is grounded in P1+P2 while P2 and A2 are written in terms of A8 — circular grounding. Independently, A3 is claimed to be Rothschild–Stiglitz screening and the signal to satisfy Spence single-crossing; both are *defined* over a common signal space, so comparability is presupposed by A3's economics, not added.

**(b) Independence from providers** — A9 (no private extraction inside the venue) plus P6 already forbid a provider gaming the manufacture of its own instrument: that is extraction. A8(1) restates A9 and A14. Derives.

**(c) Algorithmic, no discretion** — does not derive. A1(¬)+A3+P6 yield non-discrimination, not determinism: a committee applying a published rubric satisfies them and violates A8(3). Determinism is a design parameter of the same type as the demoted split ratio, and sits unreconciled against A13's "process proprietary."

Residue needing axiom status once (a) is de-duplicated: none.

## Hidden assumptions

- One risk normalisation is well-defined across heterogeneous return distributions.
- Allocators decide on that surface, not on narrative.
- Procedural determinism implies epistemic neutrality — F6 and F7 deny this.
- Independence is asserted against providers only, never against the standardiser's own revenue interest. Largest unclosed hole.
- Normalising does not deform what it measures; F9 says it does, making A8 the cause of a failure mode it should prevent.

## Load

Cited by T6, T8, T53, T54, F6, F9 and as A7's β-precondition (β = 0 without A8) — hence F2 and all propagation. No proof in §IV discharges it; the load is declared in dependency lists only. Dropping (c) breaks no theorem: T54's observability comes from A9. Dropping (a) breaks A7β, but P2 still carries it.

## Recommendation

**Split, then demote.** Keep one axiom:

*A8 (Commensurability): certification yields a per-agent measurement; allocation requires an ordering across agents; that translation is not implied by certification.*

Demote independence to a corollary of A9+P6, algorithmicity to a governance parameter. Strip the enabling clause from P2 and the manufacturing clause from A2 — otherwise the surviving axiom is analytic.
