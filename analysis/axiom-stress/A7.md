## Statement (as in spec)

Trust begets confidence (β-loop): Quotes aggregate into INDX; outsiders observe collective proof and award capital. Grounded in P3+P1+P2+A8; β = 0 without A8. α is now T56.

## Derivation attempt

A7 conflates two claims of different status.

**Weak reading — "capital responds to proof."** Near-derivable. P2 *defines* confidence as a forward-looking bet on observed P1, rational only when the instrument is comparable. A8 supplies comparability, A2+A13 the public certified proof, A3 the separating equilibrium. By that definition, "allocation increases in observed comparable credibility" is unpacking, not a new claim — the `g(C, dC/dt)` term.

**Strong reading — β > 0.** Not derivable. β is a term *separate* from g: response to the aggregate trust stock itself — positive feedback, not response to credibility. Every route fails:

- **A8** is a necessary condition (¬standardisation → K = 0). It licenses possibility, never actuality.
- **A10** types an act already conditioned on confidence, pointing inward (X→e∩X). A stipulation carries no existential import and cannot produce the *first* outside allocation.
- **A1** cuts against: allocation is decided by access, not merit. A7 asserts those allocators invert inside the comparison surface. Nothing bridges that.
- **T42, T53** consume A7 (Spence's employer-response is itself an equilibrium assumption). Circular.
- **P6** route is is/ought.

An irreducible core survives — but it is not what A7 says.

## Hidden assumptions

Comparability is *sufficient*, not merely necessary, to move capital. Allocators are unconstrained (mandate, custody, jurisdiction, track-length minima). Capital and provider inflow are one flux: Proof 17 step 1 reads "attracts confidence" as "new members enter"; A7 delivers capital, not providers. β is unbounded above, yet F7 calls that regime fatal. A7 and Proof 1 write ΔC where the equation puts β in dK/dt.

## Load

T1, T5–T8, T10, T11, T19, T31, T33, T37, T42, T45, T47, T56, T57, T58, T61, F2, F3, F7. Demoting A7α *increased* the load: T56 derives α *from* A7, so β now roots both halves of the recurrence. Drop A7 and both fail — F1 and F2 at once, e^X gone.

## Recommendation

**Restate; do not merge.** Fold the weak reading into P2/A8 as a corollary. Keep as axiom only the magnitude claim: *0 < β ≤ β_max — aggregate certified proof attracts incremental external capital at a rate bounded by the capacity ceiling (F7).* Correct ΔC → ΔK. Re-derive Proof 17 step 1 without the capital/provider equivocation.
