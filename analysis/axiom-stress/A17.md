## Statement (as in spec)

A17 — Attestation without custody (path-not-float). The protocol certifies the full money loop (allocation-in → fill → realised P&L → payout to owner) from inside the path, at arm's length from a custodian or settlement ledger, never holding the float: a read, not possession. Grounded in P1 + P6 + A2.

## Derivation attempt

**(i) Path presence — derivable, circular as written.** A2's estimator must be protocol-computable, which constrains its inputs: fix the estimand as the closed money loop and path presence follows. But A2 does not fix that scope — only T63.1 does, and T63.1 derives from A17. Move the scope clause into A2 or P1 and this half stops being axiomatic.

**(ii) Custody-independence — irreducible.** A9 forbids taking the other side and trading on aggregate flow information; a segregated custodian does neither, so A9 never reaches possession — as the spec concedes ("generalises, via A17"). The best bridge — custody is capability, and self-binding should remove capability, not intent — is prudential, not entailment, and proves too much. A13 governs information release, not possession. P6 orders reward, A14 claim. **P0–P6 contain no possession predicate** — hence underivable.

**Defect — bundling, as in the other ten:** a scope clause belonging to A2, an irreducible separation claim, a mechanism, and consequences, under one number.

## Hidden assumptions

- Custody, float, arm's length and owner are undefined at axiom level.
- Observability without possession is empirical, and a third-party custodial rail can revoke it. The anchor is assumed honest: A17 trades counterparty risk for reference-integrity risk, with no failure theorem for a lying reference.
- "Long enough to attest" is unquantified — A13's granularity and lag defect again.

## Load

T62 and T63 rest on A17 alone among the axioms; T39's "no client money" cites it. Dropping A17 collapses both theorems but not the content: A2 and A9 restate path-not-float almost verbatim — the claim is made three times. Two breaks. §0 says the normative content sits in exactly three places, yet A17 is a fourth self-binding commitment, and the only one ordering *possession*. And A17 alone is exempt from the force map. T62's core claim is empirical, not derived, with a live counterexample class: rating agencies, benchmark administrators and auditors are regulated without custody. It needs a falsifiable anchor, as A1's did (E2).

## Recommendation

**Restate and split.** The axiom is the separation claim: *"Attestation and custody are separable: certifying an economic loop requires observational access to its events, anchored against a reference the protocol does not control, and no proprietary interest in the assets moved."* Path presence → A2 as an estimand-scope clause; delete the duplicate in the A2 row. Anchor enumeration and T63's mechanism → the settlement component specification, already SHALL statements. Admit the restated A17 in §0, or correct §0's count. Add an anchor-compromise failure theorem, and a falsifiable anchor for T62 — publish it with a jurisdiction hedge, since it is a legal assertion. Do not merge into A9.
