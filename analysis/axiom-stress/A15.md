## Statement (as in spec)

The ★-center pump preserves aggregate risk-adjusted directional intent across normalise → match → size → abstract → route → certify. Netting destroys individual identity; composition is infeasible. Posited as a conservation law "analogous to energy conservation." Grounded in P4+A9.

## Derivation attempt

Three bundled claims, behaving differently.

**(1) The netting identity — derivable.** A9 makes the CCP sole resting counterparty, so no interior party absorbs flow; A4/P4 make a match a pairing of opposite signs, so matched interest sums to zero. Per cycle, Σᵢvᵢ = 0 + external residual. Bookkeeping, not physics. Well-posedness needs a commit boundary — A16. But the graph has A16←A15, so the defence is circular. Reverse that arrow and A15's core falls out as a theorem of A9+A4+A16.

**(2) Conservation across normalise/size — definitional or false.** Normalise rescales each signal by λᵢ; Σλᵢvᵢ ≠ Σvᵢ. Conservation holds only if the invariant is *defined* as the post-normalisation sum — unfalsifiable. If sizing responds to the net book (any binding capacity, margin or risk limit) the map is nonlinear and intent dies at the cap. Counter-model: a venue that normalises, nets, then truncates the residual to a house exposure limit violates A15 while satisfying A1–A14 and P6. Every capital-constrained venue does this; F7's own mitigation prescribes capacity capping. F7 and A15 are inconsistent.

**(3) "certify" is not a map on the intent space** — its codomain is ±ΔINDX per provider. Conservation across it is undefined without a projection.

**(4) Composition-infeasibility is neither conservation nor cryptographic.** Unnoised sum aggregation gives no security guarantee: an (N−1) coalition, or anyone differencing the aggregate across cycles, recovers a member's signal. It is a falsifiable precondition on cohort size and observability; its dependence on batch length (T64) is the tell: engineering, not invariant.

## Hidden assumptions

Intent is additive (fails for options, contingent orders); no binding capacity limit; λᵢ exogenous to the net book; routed = filled; cohort large, no cross-cycle differencing; one batch window.

## Load

Thin. Only A16, T64 and the stock-flow principle cite it; no proof does. T64 survives on A4+T2+T18 (a longer window is a larger effective N). A16 stands alone. Nothing collapses.

## Recommendation

Split. Demote (1) to a theorem of A9+A4+A16 and reverse A16←A15: atomicity is the primitive commitment. Restate (4) as a design requirement with a minimum-cohort threshold. Drop "certify" from the transform list or name the projection; reconcile with F7.
