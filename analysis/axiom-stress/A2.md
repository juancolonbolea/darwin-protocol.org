## Statement (as in spec)

Merit can be certified — certified at every touchpoint: independently measured, publicly attested, externally validated, attributed across the value chain. Grounded P1+P6.

## Derivation attempt

Four of five conjuncts are carried elsewhere: independently measured → A8 (arm's-length, algorithmic); publicly attested → A13; externally validated → A4+A7; attributed per contributor → A15+A16; normative force → P6.

Every route to the fifth stalls:

- **P1** already says credibility is "certified by the protocol" — A2 then follows analytically. A defect, not a derivation: the primitive does axiom work.
- **A8+A13+P6** give a comparable, published, arm's-length number, not a *valid* one. Nothing forces the score to track contribution — the Quote is no sufficient statistic. Comparability ≠ estimability.
- **A3** certifies a binary type, not a continuous magnitude, and presupposes quality-responsive allocation.
- **A15/A16, A7** — circular. A16 ← A15+T42, T42 ← A2. A7's antecedent ("proven edge") already contains A2.

**Residual:** contribution is *identifiable from record* — separable from luck, beta and access at finite sample, robust to being optimised against. A1: merit does not allocate; P6: it should; A2: it can be seen. Non-redundant, unstated.

**A2 vs A8.** A8 defends itself against A2; A2 never defends against A8 and absorbs it ("certification includes manufacturing"). Containment, not partition — the pattern that demoted A6 into A7.

## Hidden assumptions

Skill is detectable at protocol horizons — the largest empirical bet. Merit stays stationary into the allocation period (F7/F9 concede failure; A2 states none). The measure survives optimisation against it — F9 is A2's exposure, charged to A8. Joint output decomposes into per-actor shares (T44/T59). The external market is a merit oracle — but A1 says markets allocate by access.

## Load

T1, T11, T23, T31–T33, T41–T44, T47, T49, T53, T56, F6; A16 via T42. Without A2: T53 unravels, T47/T56 fail (α unproven → F1 → e^X becomes e×X), T42 loses its object, double-entry INDX collapses — costlier than A6+A11+A12 combined. Yet downstream proofs use only the thin claim, never "every touchpoint" or "externally validated" (T42 supplies that, else circular).

## Recommendation

**Restate**, deleting absorbed conjuncts.

> **A2 — Merit is estimable.** There exists a protocol-computable estimator of contribution separating it from luck, beta and access at finite sample, informative even when participants know it is computed. Comparability is A8; publication A13; attribution A15+A16; external check T42.

Strip "certified by the protocol" from P1, or A2 remains analytic.
