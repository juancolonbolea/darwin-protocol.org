## Strongest counter-argument (steelman)

A16's foundational residue is not atomicity but **quantisation**: trust minting is discrete and per-provider attributable — INDX cannot be minted continuously. Three places quantify over a cycle no other axiom brings into existence: T64 treats cycle length as a free variable; T42's bilateral extension posts "per-cycle per-provider ledger entries"; Stock-Flow Conservation integrates INDX "across cycles." If the cycle is undefined, all three range over nothing. A16 is the only axiom that posits it — and §6 of a companion document names A16 by hand as what makes the trust ledger well-defined.

## Does it succeed? No.

1. **A15 already quantises.** Its conserved quantity is the *vector sum of all members' risk-adjusted signals* — a sum defined only over a determinate membership set, i.e. a batch. Discreteness is A15's precondition, not A16's contribution. A16's only novel content is all-or-nothing commit: a durability property of an implementation, not of the economics.
2. **The load is discharged upstream.** T42's own text asserts per-cycle, per-provider, two-sided INDX entries and derives from A2+A4+A7+A13. Stock-Flow Conservation derives the ledger from A15+T42+definitional accounting. A16 is grounded in A15+T42 — the *same parents*. It is a sibling of what it claims to enable, not a parent.
3. **Attribution derives from P6+A2+T44**, not commit semantics.

## Recommendation

Demote as originally recommended. Two repairs the original card missed: the by-name citation in a companion document §6 must be rewritten to A15+T42, or demotion leaves a dangling reference; and the original's circularity route is overstated — axioms grounded on axioms are routine here (A7←A8, A8←A9), so only grounding on a *theorem* is anomalous. Also reconcile A16's "double-entry" against "triple-entry" elsewhere, and the §II force table's omission of A15/A16 from S.
