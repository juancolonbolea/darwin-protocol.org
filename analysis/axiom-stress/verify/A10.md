## Strongest counter-argument (steelman)

A5 reinvests *surplus* — rescued mistrust cost from matching. It never says accrued **trust stock** is redeployable; P1–P3 type trust as a stock but grant it no allocative power. So "allocate trust stock → mint further trust" needs a premise, and A10 is the only candidate: **X→e∩X** is the sole arrow running from the capital circle back into the trust overlap by an *act of allocation*, and "≥ e" is the only required return on trust anywhere in the spec. Kill it and T9 is groundless, Proof 9 step 3 unsupported, and the capstone chain T35→T36→T37/T39/T40 loses its flow leg. Proof 18 adds a second use: member skin-in-the-game is *insider* self-allocation, which A7's outsider β does not cover.

## Does it succeed? No.

1. The premise is exactly what A10 withholds. A10 presupposes trust-stock redeployability; it never asserts it. The card's central finding survives contact.
2. Proof 9 consumes `= e`, not A10's `≥ e` belief. A5's Compound vector supplies organic compounding *at rate e* — an equality, and stronger. A5 discharges the step better than A10 does.
3. The spec's own load test: every force-powering axiom carries a negation-keyed failure theorem (¬T56, ¬A7, ¬A9/¬A4/¬A14). A10 appears in none of F1–F9, in no cross-validation row, in no open gap. Its falsity produces no pathology.
4. Force mapping survives. Inheritance is already one axiom plus one theorem (A5, T56); demoting A10 gives Propagation the identical shape (A7, T9). The temporal view is unchanged.
5. Proofs 18/19/21 cite A10 only parenthetically beside A7; the inference is carried by A7 plus A13/A2. T57's declared dependencies omit A10 though its proof cites it — bookkeeping drift, not load.

## Recommendation

DERIVABLE stands; demote as the original card advises. One correction to that card: the act/metric equivocation is committed by Proof 9 (steps 3→4) and by T35's "P3 = flow" parenthetical, which contradicts §I and §IX typing trust as a stock. A10's own text disclaims metric status. Charge that defect to the proofs, and repair the typing while demoting.
