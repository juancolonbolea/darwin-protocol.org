## Statement (as in spec)

Trust-flow is confidence-loaded allocation: allocating trust stock (P3) confident it will yield further trust at rate ≥ e. "Not a metric — the act." Grounded P3+P2, circle X→e∩X, force P alongside A7.

## Derivation attempt

**A10 is a stipulative definition.** Its form is copular. It introduces *trust-flow* — a term appearing in no primitive and no other axiom — and fixes the referent in already-primitive vocabulary. Definitions constrain nothing; they are eliminable by substitution.

**The substantive residue derives.** (1) A5 asserts surplus is reinvested *wherever it mints the most trust*, each cycle netting positive when e > 0; argmax is strictly stronger than a "≥ e" hurdle. (2) A5 is itself grounded in P3, so trust stock already sits inside A5's domain — it is the Compound vector. (3) P2 *defines* confidence as the forward-looking bet accompanying any allocation to certified credibility; every allocation act is confidence-loaded by P2, not by a further posit. (4) Set trust-flow := A5's reinvestment act restricted to the P3 argument. By (3) it is confidence-loaded; by (1) its expected yield clears e. ∎

**The one place it strains.** Step (3) identifies two relations sharing the name *confidence*: P2's (capital → certified credibility) and A10's (trust stock → further trust). Bridging them needs *trust stock is redeployable as allocative capital*. That P3↔X fungibility claim is irreducible — and A10 nowhere asserts it. A10 conceals it rather than carries it.

## Hidden assumptions

P3↔X fungibility, unstated. *Trust-flow* carries three non-equivalent referents: dT/dt from the product rule (calculus, not axiom); A10's deployment act; and `e × X` in the stock/flow proof — which additionally converts A10's *believed* ≥ e into a *realised* = e without warrant. And the hurdle is a belief with no truth condition and no downstream consequence: unfalsifiable as written.

## Load

Sole exclusive dependent: T9 → T35 → T36/T37/T39/T40. Across all 21 proofs A10 appears only co-cited with A7 ("A7 β + A10"); every such step's content is "capital flows to proven edge," which is A7 alone. Delete A10 and no step changes. T9 re-derives from A5+P3. Nothing collapses.

## Recommendation

**Demote.** Move the definition to the vocabulary layer; absorb the reinvestment content into A5. If an axiom is wanted at this junction, state the claim actually in use: *"Accrued trust stock is redeployable as allocative capital."* That is irreducible. A10 is not.
