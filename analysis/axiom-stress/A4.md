## Statement (as in spec)

"Matching eliminates friction across five dimensions. Any pairing that removes friction creates surplus. Five dimensions: flow, knowledge, capital, community, tool. Matching is a by-product of community activity (P4), not the goal. The friction eliminated IS the cost of mistrust (P0)."

## Derivation attempt

Four clauses. C1 (the five dimensions) and C2 (by-product, not goal) appear verbatim in P4. C3 (friction = P0's cost) is P0's own definition. C1–C3 are restatement; they add nothing.

Only C4 — friction removal *creates* surplus rather than relocating it — is candidate axiomatic content. Here the set contradicts itself: the audit trail demotes the former surplus primitive as "derivable from P4 (matching produces surplus)." If that stands, P4 carries C4 and A4 is redundant. If not, the demotion was unsound. Exactly one survives; the spec asserts both.

The honest chain — P4 (matching occurs) + P0 (the unmatched state carries cost) ⊢ surplus — needs two provisos absent from every axiom and primitive: that the rescued cost is deadweight rather than payment for a service, and that matching costs less than it removes. The derivation fails there, and so does the demotion's.

## Hidden assumptions

1. **Positive-sum (normative).** The spec's own pump reading calls flow surplus extraction from external rent-takers — a transfer across a welfare boundary drawn to exclude them.
2. **Net-of-cost (empirical).** No operating-cost term exists anywhere.
3. **No congestion.** "Any pairing" is unrestricted, yet the credibility-optimal internal-match ratio is 70–85%, not 1 — more matching stops paying. T18's filtering also presumes some pairings are negative.
4. **Scaling in N.** A4 never mentions N; T2 and T26 read density growth out of it.
5. **Dimensional independence.** Required by T29's product form and the regulatory-separation theorem's "independently regulable." Unstated.
6. **Exhaustiveness of five.** Empirical enumeration.

## Load

T1, T2, T7, T12, T18, T24, T26, T27, T28, T29, T41, T42, T44, T45, T47, T51, T55, T56, T57, F4; A8, A9, A15 lean on it. Dropping A4 costs nothing P0+P4 do not already supply. What collapses is its *unstated* content — scaling and separability — which no axiom carries.

## Recommendation

**Restate and split.** Proposed: "Matching over the five dimensions removes P0 friction at a cost below the friction removed, up to a per-dimension capacity beyond which marginal pairings are non-positive. The dimensions are separable." Move scaling to a density lemma T2/T26/T29 can cite. Drop C1–C3. Reconcile with the surplus-primitive demotion.
