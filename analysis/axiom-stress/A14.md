## Statement (as in spec)

"Individuals at the table, not on the menu. The venue IS the union." The intent inversion rendered as governance rule. Grounded P4+P6; claims to resolve Holmström moral hazard.

## Derivation attempt

A14 fuses two claims of unequal logical status.

**"Not on the menu" derives.** A9 makes the CCP sole resting counterparty and bans member limit orders — no member can occupy the predator role. A9's own information reading already guarantees "no one acts on aggregate flow information, no privileged counterparty information, no extraction from internal dynamics knowledge." A15 destroys individual identity through netting, making the aggregate composition-infeasible. With A4's matching, A4+A9+A15 ⇒ individual intent is unexploitable inside. The published independence defence — a venue could satisfy A9 yet still let privileged actors front-run member intent — is refuted by A9's own text, which names that channel. Two further redundancies: A14's mechanism clause restates A9's CCP clause verbatim; the Holmström resolution is performed by A8+A9 in T54, which never cites A14.

**"At the table / the venue IS the union" does not derive.** A9 bans extraction, not rule — a non-extractive autocrat satisfies every other axiom. A3 gives uniform terms, not standing; P6 orders reward, never control. This half is irreducible, but unstated: A14's grounding (P4) argues only for the derivable half.

## Hidden assumptions

- **N-dependence.** Identity destruction needs netting depth; at low N, aggregate intent ≈ one member's. Asserted unconditionally.
- **Scope.** A9 is scoped inside the venue; F8 needs A14 to bind outside.
- **Merit→governance.** P6 governs reward; "the venue IS the union" is a control claim beyond it.
- **Benign observer.** Full observability is both the protection and the channel for reading intent; operator trustworthiness is assumed, not derived.

## Load

T12 (A4+A9+A14) is near-circular — the theorem restates the axiom — and survives on A4+A9+A15, carrying T13/T14/T20/T21/T25/T48. T43's edge-side step is substitutable by A2+P6, both already cited. F4 lists ¬A14 but fires on ¬A9 alone. F8 (external capture) is the sole non-redundant load, resting entirely on the underivable half.

## Recommendation

**Split.** Demote protection to a theorem (A4+A9+A15). Restate the residual: "**A14 (Standing).** Members hold governance standing and the residual claim on the venue; obligations to members are not contingent on external power." Ground in P6+A3. Note the rendering asymmetry: the data inversion is rendered primitive-plus-theorem, the intent inversion as an axiom. Method-output is not axiomatic; only the standing claim passes.
