## Statement (as in spec)

No private extraction inside the venue. CCP is sole resting counterparty; no member limit orders internally; all internal surplus mutualized. Fiscal clause: rescued spread has "exactly two destinations" — dataset acquisition, processor funding. Grounded P4+P6, force S.

## Derivation attempt

**Best chain (A14+P4+P6).** A14 inverts intent asymmetry and its own text names "CCP sole counterparty" as the inversion. P4 makes matching a by-product of members' own flow, so an interposed counterparty supplies nothing. P6 then denies it reward. A14 and A9 carry the *identical* grounding pair (P4+P6) and the identical ★ circle.

**Where it fails.** The chain yields "extraction is unmerited," not "extraction is impossible." A14 gives members governance voice; nothing in P4/P6 stops a member-governed venue from voting to extract (the demutualisation path). That residue — the venue self-binding against its own governors — is not derivable. So A9 is not DERIVABLE.

**Nor cleanly independent.** Three claim-types are bundled: (1) a *mechanism* (sole resting counterparty, no internal limit orders, a named book) — implementation, demotable exactly as the 75/25 ratio was; (2) a *normative prohibition* already carried by P6 and by force C, whose row reads "merit, not extraction, determines outcomes" and is powered by P6+A1(¬), not A9; (3) a *fiscal clause* colliding with T21, which declares allocation a governance surface. Neither non-redundant piece is axiom-shaped.

## Hidden assumptions

Liquidity provision is never a contribution (else P6 permits paying for it). Mutualised governance resists capture. Net take-rate is zero — contradicted by the Take-rate Ceiling, which permits a bounded positive take, making the binding content a *parameter*. And the transition note concedes A9 is currently false by design.

## Load

Direct: T12, T14, T19, T20, T22, T32, T34, T41, T51, T55, T59, T61, F4, plus A8 and A15. Load-bearing: T14 and T22 collapse outright; T51's non-rivalry boundary loses enforcement, so e^X degrades to e×X; A8's unbiased-standardiser premise fails, taking β with it. Decorative: T32, T34, T55. F4 survives via its ¬A4/¬A14 disjuncts. T12 and T14 have no proofs.

## Recommendation

**Split and restate.** Keep one prohibition: *"No party, the operator included, may take the other side of member flow or trade on aggregate flow information."* Demote the venue mechanics to the mechanism layer and the two-destinations clause to governance under T21. Re-assign A9 to force C, and amend the claim that P6 is the only normative primitive — A9's residue is a second one.
