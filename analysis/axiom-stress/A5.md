## Statement (as in spec)
Surplus (rescued information-asymmetry cost) reinvested wherever it mints most trust → more trust → more surplus; when e > 0 each cycle yields more than it consumes. Vectors: Compound, Source, Develop.

## Derivation attempt
Tested: A4 (surplus exists) + A9 (no extraction) + A10 (trust-flow allocates trust stock expecting ≥ e) ⇒ A5. Three breaks.

1. **A9 is a prohibition, not a directive.** Mutualised surplus is equally satisfied by rebate or idle reserve — closure, not destination. A9's "exactly two destinations" clause is A5 content written into A9; question-begging.
2. **Register mismatch.** A4's surplus is economic; A10 governs trust stock. The bridge — "trust-flow is the reinvestment act" — is T9, derived from A10+A5. Circular.
3. **Modality.** A10's "≥ e" is ex-ante confidence; A5 asserts ex-post realisation. Read fact-typed, A10 already carries compounding — A5 then duplicates A10, not A4+A9.

Nothing bounds marginal reinvestment return at ≥ 1, nor supplies the argmax's cross-vector metric (T17 formalises it, but derives from A5). A7+T56 already yield e^X without reinvestment.

## Hidden assumptions
- Marginal trust return ≥ 1; sourcing cost below lifetime matching surplus.
- e > 0 is exogenous; unsecured by the set.
- A competent allocator with unbiased expectations.
- Normative: compounding over rebate — P6 permits rebating by merit.
- Trust minting is ex-ante comparable across vectors.
- The vectors exhaust reinvestment; economic surplus and trust stock are fungible.

## Load
Direct: T1, T3, T5, T8, T9, T15–T17, T25, T28, T37–T38, T46, T48, T58, F3. Dropping it collapses the S-curve/allocation/governance chain (T15–T17, T21, T36, T40), T25, T48, T3's growth engine. e^X survives — the T5 proof derives exponentiality from T56+A7 without invoking A5. A5 is the only force-carrying axiom no failure theorem negates.

## Recommendation
**Split.** Axiom keeps only retention: surplus is redeployed inside the venue, not distributed or held — the directive complement to A9; strip "exactly two destinations" from A9 to stop cross-loading. Demote compounding to a conditional tied to external validation, the vectors to definition, the argmax to governance (T21 already treats the ratio as a parameter). Also fix T5's dependencies; add a ¬A5 failure mode (surplus rebated or idle → linear S-curve).

Proposed: "Rescued surplus is retained and redeployed into trust-minting capacity, not distributed or held. Where marginal trust return ≥ 1 and e > 0, each cycle yields more than it consumes. Allocation across vectors is governance."
