## Statement (as in spec)
"Fair terms make self-selection total. Same deal for everyone. Fee gates tourists. Publication gates frauds." Claimed to *be* Rothschild-Stiglitz screening; four cost dimensions (time, fee, drawdown, publication) said to satisfy Spence single-crossing.

## Derivation attempt
**(a) Uniformity — irreducible.** From A1+P6: if access-privilege is the pathology and contribution should determine reward, differentiated entry terms are access-privilege. Fails: P6 *licenses* merit-indexed terms — a fee tiered on certified quality is contribution-determined. Only A3 forbids tiering.

**(b) Screening — derivable.** A2 (with its A8 manufacturing clause) + A13 + P1 already produce a costly public signal whose time and drawdown cost falls in type — single-crossing. T53's Spence argument runs on P1+A2+A13 alone; A3 is an input there but supplies only (a).

**(c) Totality — contradicted.** Nothing proves a separating equilibrium exists. RS cuts *against* A3: RS is *menu* screening — several contracts, so types sort. A3 offers one: in RS terms the pooling contract, cream-skimmable by a rival offering tiered terms. RS does not make A3 a theorem of A2+A13; it exposes uniformity as resting on an unstated no-competing-venue premise.

## Hidden assumptions
- A flat fee has dc/dθ = 0. It screens on wealth and outside option, not quality — A1 reasserting itself at the gate.
- Providers know their own type. Under overconfidence the fee taxes tourists rather than deterring them; "total" fails.
- No rival venue offers merit-tiered terms.
- An unnamed entry-cost parameter. F5 ("A3 over-optimised") and F8 ("no viable common cost") are claims about that vector's *level*; uniformity has no level, so neither is expressible in A3's terms.
- Published results reveal fraud — F6 is the set's own counterexample.

## Load
T43 (edge-side premise), T53, F5/F6/F8 by negation. Thin: A2 already asserts the protocol "enforces fair measurement and reward," covering T43's edge side, and T53 survives on P1+A2+A13. No positive theorem collapses.

## Recommendation
**Split.** Demote screening into T53; keep uniformity as a venue constraint (A9's species — no discrimination inside, none at the boundary); add the calibration.

Proposed: *"A3 — Uniform Terms. One contract, offered to every prospective provider. Entry is not gated by negotiated terms, discretionary underwriting, capital minimum, or prior standing, and terms are not indexed to certified quality. Uniformity constrains the venue; it asserts nothing about who enters. The entry-cost vector is a governance parameter bounded below by fraud deterrence, above by variation supply."*
