## Statement (as in spec)
Allocation of capital, work, and ownership is decided less by contribution than by access. Trading is the first instance; labour and capital markets follow. Access is information asymmetry made structural. Grounded in P0+P6.

## Derivation attempt
Four claims. (i) Asymmetry exists in these markets — P0 instantiated. (ii) Asymmetry-driven allocation departs from merit — analytic once P6 is added, P6 being defined as P0's normative inverse. (i)+(ii) derive, and the spec concedes it: A1 is "the two primitives held next to each other." (iii) Access *dominates* merit. (iv) The pattern generalises across domains and self-reproduces. Neither derives.

Chains tried, and where each breaks.
- **A2+A13 (Akerlof).** Unobservable quality yields pooling or unravelling — that is ¬merit. It never names the *substitute* allocator. The complement of merit includes noise, luck, and pooling; A1 asserts one specific, persistent one. Sharpest failure.
- **A8.** Without standardisation K=0. That predicts non-allocation, not misallocation by access; and A8's necessity claim is internal to the protocol's own construction.
- **A3.** Uniform terms suffice to screen; the absence of uniform terms does not entail access-dominance.
- **A9/F4.** Extraction concerns the surplus split, not who receives allocation. Category error.
- **A4** already asserts friction exists and is monetisable, covering A1's only deductive contribution to T1.

## Hidden assumptions
Dominance magnitude (no metric, no falsifier). Non-erosion: access advantage is never competed away. Separability: access is not itself accumulated past contribution. Domain transfer to labour and ownership, asserted only. Agency: "decided"/"rigged" imports culpability P0 does not license. Circular yardstick: A1 measures the world against a merit-ordering that A2+A8 only manufacture downstream.

## Load
Cited by exactly one theorem (T1), the V force, C (negation), and diagnostically in F5. Appears in none of the 21 proofs — the least-loaded active axiom. T1's dynamics come from A2+A4+A5+A7; dropping A1 costs T1 nothing formal. Rhetorically it carries everything. Maximal narrative load, near-zero deductive load is the signature of the demoted axioms. F5 also needs A1 as a universally quantified *law* ("incumbents become the new access barrier") while A1's text is a particular fact — an unlicensed use.

## Recommendation
**Split.** Retain A1 as a law: *"Where information asymmetry is structural and unevenly distributed, allocation converges on access rather than contribution, and the advantage is self-reproducing."* Move the dominance-and-domain claim out of the axiom set into a second load-bearing empirical anchor beside T42, with a stated measurement and falsification condition.
