## Statement (as in spec)

Results public, IP encrypted, process proprietary. Individual selfishness → social trust. Grounded in P1+P6; the credibility inversion — P7 applied to P0's credibility dimension.

## Derivation attempt

Three rules plus a consequence. Each falls.

**1. Results public.** A2 already reads "independently measured, *publicly attested*, externally validated" — so the axiom page's defence ("A2 says nothing about what must be made public") contradicts A2's own text. Un-gated then follows from A1+P6: a gate on the quality signal is itself an access privilege.

**2. IP encrypted.** A3's single-crossing condition (dc/dθ < 0) needs signal cost falling in quality. Disclosing the strategy makes cost *rise* with quality — better edge, more surrendered. Single-crossing breaks and T53's separating equilibrium collapses. Over-determined by A9's firewall reading.

**3. Process proprietary.** Not derivable, and contradicted: T30, derived *from* A13, concludes open methods strengthen the moat; A8 requires an auditable standardiser.

**4. "Selfishness → social trust"** is T11's conclusion verbatim, and T11 ← A2+A7+A13. The axiom asserts the theorem that cites it.

**On P7.** A13 is the output of a stated operation over stated inputs, so it reduces to them; the embedded test ("does trust emerge? yes") is empirical validation inside an axiom. A14 shares the structure.

**Where every chain fails.** None fixes the disclosure *boundary*: what, at what granularity, with what lag. Real-time positions would destroy the edge published. That residue is the only axiomatic content, and A13 omits it.

## Hidden assumptions

- Publication does not degrade the edge disclosed — F9, the spec's own monoculture failure, is assumed away.
- The published result is a sufficient quality signal; A8's informativeness note denies it.
- Disclosure is individually rational at every quality level.
- "Public" means costless, symmetric access: no latency or rate tiers.

## Load

T11, T27, T28, T30, T31, T34, T42, T47, T49, T53, T54, T56, T57 — every proof uses it for one job, "results public → observable," which A2 supplies. Absorbing conjuncts 1–2 breaks no proof; V retains A1–A3, so F5 is unchanged. The axiom page also claims A13 grounds T1; the dependency graph disagrees.

## Recommendation

Split and retire. Fold conjunct 1 into A2 ("publicly attested" → "un-gated"); conjunct 2 into A3 as the single-crossing precondition; delete conjunct 3 and the closing sentence. If a slot is kept, restate as: **"Certified results are disclosed at a granularity and lag that preserves the disclosed edge's capacity."**
