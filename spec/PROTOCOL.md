## §10. Open Trust — The Resolution of P0

P0 is information asymmetry. Open Trust is its resolution — not a step in a priority stack, but the mechanism that makes every other primitive work.

### Open Trust inverts P0 from cost to revenue

P0 says information asymmetry is a COST (the spread). Open Trust says information SYMMETRY is REVENUE (network surplus share). The inversion makes P0's cost into Open Trust's revenue. The spread that extractors collect from opacity becomes the surplus that contributors earn from transparency.

This is the deepest inversion: not resolving P0 (removing the cost), but FLIPPING P0 (turning the cost into income). Every dollar of spread that previously leaked to intermediaries becomes a dollar of surplus available to members who contribute to symmetry.

### Effect on the trust equation

```
dT/dt = (dC/dt)·K + C·(dK/dt)
```

The three information inversions (§3.1) each accelerate ONE term:
- Credibility inversion → accelerates dC/dt (more edge certified per cycle)
- Intent inversion → protects K (capital doesn't leak to extractors)
- Data inversion → accelerates dK/dt (more confidence from open proof)

Open Trust acts on BOTH terms simultaneously because each of its four axes fuels one of the four operational forces directly:
- More disclosure → more C created (providers contribute to dataset → more certified edge)
- More disclosure → more K attracted (transparency → institutional confidence → capital inflow)
- Open Trust acts across dT/dt globally, not per-term

This is why **Open Trust is the operational interface to V→S→I→P** (T57, [[CONSTITUTION]]): each disclosure axis fuels one evolutionary force by bijection, not a scalar multiplier on any primitive. The engine (V/S/I/P) and the interface (Data/Noise/Timing/Capital) are the same system viewed at different layers.

### The formal logic of redistribution

**Open Trust inverts information economics.** Traditional finance: secrecy = profit. DARWIN economy: openness = profit. Disclosed signals improve matching (higher IMR), improve selection (better INDX), and grow trust stock (more X). The surplus generated flows back to the discloser proportionally.

**The disclosure gradient — four axes, bijective onto V/S/I/P (T57, canonical mapping):**

**The four-axis gradient is isomorphic to the V/S/I/P basis.** Data ↔ V (disclosed signals = additions to the variation pool, via A13+P5). Noise ↔ S (ε clarity = selection sharpness, via A8+T18).
