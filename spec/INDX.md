> **Depth 0 — Pitch. All audiences.**

Mechanisms: P5 (the dataset) + T30 (open methods → dataset moat)

Datasets appreciate — every trade, every match, every outcome enriches the collective record. By publishing methods openly (T30), Darwinex accelerates community learning while the dataset — 100K+ track records, matching patterns, risk-adjusted histories — compounds as an irreversible moat.

The inversion resolves Ostrom's information externality (Nobel 2009): data treated as commons, governed by the protocol, available to all members. The result is not charity — it is the mechanism by which trust produces edge.

> **The data inversion is load-bearing.** Without it, trust produces confidence (via visibility) but not edge. Only the financial loop operates. The equation becomes linear (e×X). The dataset is what makes compounding exponential (e^X). See §9.

### §2. The Master Equation

> **Depth 1 — Strategic. Board, management, staff.**

Everything Darwinex does reduces to one equation:

```
X(t+1) = X(t) × (1 + e(X)) + ΔC(X) + Nτ
```

| Term | What it means | What produces it |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| **X(t)** | Trust stock at time t | All capital at risk: accumulated proof + deployed bets |
| **e(X)** | Edge rate | Risk-adjusted performance — a function of trust, not constant |
| **ΔC(X)** | Confidence flow | Forward-looking reinvestment — also a function of trust |
| **Nτ** | Membership surplus | N members × net subscription revenue |

**Why the equation matters to the board:** Both `e` and `ΔC` are functions of X. This is not accounting — it is a recurrence relation where the output of one period is the input to the next, and the growth rate itself accelerates with scale. The system does not grow at a fixed rate. It grows faster as it grows.

**The temporal structure:**
- **e** is backward-looking: proven credibility (what was demonstrated)
- **X** is present: accumulated stock (what exists now)
- **ΔC** is forward-looking: bets on future growth (what is expected)

The equation maps present to future, mediated by backward proof and forward confidence. Every operational decision at Darwinex either grows e, grows ΔC, or grows N. There is no fourth lever.

**The edge rate unpacked — the 4-D form.** The scalar e(X) hides the scoring engine. At the strategy level it decomposes:

```
e(X) = 𝟙[RiskEngine(X) = pass]  ·  Φ(O, P, Per, Cap)
Φ(O, P, Per, Cap) = O^a · P^b · Per^c · Cap^d      (a + b + c + d normalized; weights tunable by maturity)
```

Two pieces, two roles:

- **The indicator** `𝟙[RiskEngine = pass]` is the **legitimacy gate** — the operational form of P6 / C at strategy level. Multiplicative because conservation is binary: either merit is preserved or the strategy is ejected. A failing Risk Engine zeros the whole product.
- **The power-weighted Φ** is the **surplus distribution** across the four scoring dimensions. Power weights (not sums) because the dimensions substitute poorly: high Originality cannot compensate for zero Capacity, and vice versa. Geometric mean behaviour penalizes strategies that are strong on few axes and weak on others.

**Four dimensions pricing four axioms.** INDX's scoring dimensions are not a menu — they are the output-side dual of Open Trust's disclosure axes (see [[PROTOCOL]] §10.2, T58):

### §3. The Double Recurrence

> **Depth 1 — Strategic. Board, management, staff.**

The master equation becomes exponential through a single mechanism: trust produces BOTH of its own growth drivers.

**A7 (β) + T56 (α) — The Double Recurrence:**

```
Loop α (Trust → Edge):       X → dataset → e(X)       [the knowledge loop — theorem T56]
Loop β (Trust → Confidence):  X → visible proof → ΔC(X)  [the financial loop — axiom A7]
```

The two loops have different formal status after the 19 Apr revision. **Loop β is the axiom (A7):** the irreducible behavioural claim that proven, comparable, transparent edge rationally attracts capital. No prior principle derives it; it is assumed. **Loop α is a theorem (T56):** aggregate trust → edge is derived from A7 + matching + dataset + selection (specialisation of T47's G16 resolution). Individual α remains empirical, but aggregate α is proven.

**Loop α — The Knowledge Loop.** Trust enriches the dataset (P5). The dataset improves edge through five channels: inheritance corpus, matching pattern intelligence, risk engine training, selection pressure, community insight. More trust → richer dataset → better aggregate selection → higher aggregate edge → more trust. Vehicle: the dataset.

**Loop β — The Financial Loop.** Trust creates visible, auditable proof (INDX quote). External observers see this proof and allocate confidence (capital). More trust → more visible proof → more confidence → more trust. Vehicle: INDX.

**Why this matters:** In a single-loop system, growth is linear — one variable drives the other at a fixed rate. In a double-loop system, both drivers grow with the stock they produce. The growth rate itself is a function of scale:

```
e = e(X)     — edge grows with trust stock (via dataset)
ΔC = ΔC(X)   — confidence grows with trust stock (via proof)
```

The consequence is exponential compounding — not because anyone decided to call it exponential, but because the algebra forces it. Double recurrence is the structural reason e^X is literal, not aspirational.

### §4. e^X Is Literal

> **Depth 1 — Strategic. Board, staff, community.**

e^X is not a brand name. It is the algebraic structure of the system.

**T5 — Derivation:**

```
e = platform effect (supply-side)
X = network effect (demand-side)
e^X = platform effect raised to the power of network effect
```

**Platform effect (e):** As N grows, rails get cheaper per user and quality rises (dataset improves). Edge is a function of scale: `e = f(N, X)`. This is the supply-side engine — the cost of producing credibility falls while its quality increases.

**Network effect (X):** As N grows, matching density rises and trust accumulates. Trust stock is a function of participation: `X = f(members, investors, profits)`. This is the demand-side engine — the value of participating increases with the number of participants.

**The critical insight:** Both e and X are driven by the same underlying variable (N, membership scale). Platform and network effects are not independent forces — they are two expressions of the same growth dynamic. The notation e^X captures this coupling: the base (edge rate) and the exponent (trust stock) compound together.

**e^X = the steep section of the spiral.** In the spiral geometry ([[PROTOCOL]] §1, [[CONSTITUTION]] §Force-Axiom Mapping), e^X describes the phase where each V→S→I→P cycle tightens the circles dramatically — exponential convergence. At the terminus, when IMR → 100% and the circles are concentric, the spiral flattens: growth becomes linear at rate e, the system is in steady state (e×X), spread = 0. The exponential is the journey; the linear is the destination. Wall Street starved — not fought, starved.

| **Player** | Developing e | Earning first INDX |

### §5. The IMR-Split

> **Depth 1 — Strategic. Board, management, staff.**

Trust production decomposes into what stays and what leaks. The Internal Matching Ratio (IMR) is the single variable that controls the transition from organizational capital (×) to exponential compounding (^).

**From [[PROTOCOL]] §6 — The Trust Equation:**

```
dT/dt = (dC/dt)·K + C·(dK/dt)
```

Trust is minted through two simultaneous terms: new credibility × existing confidence, and existing credibility × new confidence. The matching mechanism (★) is "the × sign" — without it, trust equals zero.

**The IMR Decomposition:**

```
dT_community/dt = IMR × [(dC/dt)·K + C·(dK/dt)]     internal — compounds
dT_external/dt  = (1-IMR) × (dC/dt)·K                external — extracts
```

| IMR value | Meaning | Economic regime |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| IMR low | Most trust minted leaks to external market | Extraction economy (traditional broker) |
| IMR moderate | Community retains enough to grow | Transition (current phase) |
| IMR → 1 | Community = Market. All trust compounds internally | Endgame (mutualization) |

**The × → ^ transition:** When IMR is low, the system produces trust linearly (organizational capital multiplies inputs at a fixed rate). As IMR rises, more output recirculates as input. At IMR → 1, the system's output IS its input — compounding becomes exponential because there is no leakage.

**Connection to [[REWARD]]:** The matching engine's job is to raise IMR — every internal match is a leak prevented, a unit of trust that compounds instead of escaping. The ★ circle (REWARD) is the operational implementation of the IMR-split. The X circle (INDX) is the mathematical consequence.

**Connection to the three-circle geometry:** The X circle is the outermost of the three circles (e, ★, X). It represents the societal layer — where trust becomes a public good. IMR measures how concentric the circles are: at IMR 0%, the circles barely touch (full P0); at IMR 100%, they are concentric (P0 = 0). As the X circle grows (more trust compounded, more dataset shared, more confidence attracted), it pulls the other two inward — because a larger trust stock makes the e circle more productive (α-loop, T56: trust → edge via aggregated P5) and makes ★ more efficient (more flow → more matching density). Concentricity is the visual expression of X(t+1) = X(t)×(1+e(X)): the trust stock growing at a rate that is itself a function of the trust stock. See [[REWARD]] §4 for the three-circle definitions and impact hierarchy, [[BOARD_DECK_FRAMEWORK]] §0 for the visual primitive.

### §7. The S-Curve and the Waterfall

> **Depth 2 — Operational. Management, staff.**

The system's trajectory is not improvised. It follows a prescribed S-curve toward a mathematically determined fixed point — and the layers of compounding unlock sequentially like a waterfall.

**The S-Curve (T16):**

| Phase | Distance from X* | Growth rate | Dominant driver |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Launch** | Far (vast gap) | g >> e | Sourcing (ΔN/N > e). Membership growth dominates. |
| **Growth** | Closing | g > e, declining | e inflection (T10). Dataset goes real-time. Steepest part of curve. |
| **Maturity** | Near (gap ≈ 0) | g → e | Compounding dominates. Organic rate. Steady state. |

**The e inflection (T10) — the steepest moment:** When the community reaches sufficient matching density, real-time edge-weighted sentiment supersedes historical dataset learning as the primary driver of e. Before: members learn from past trades (lagging). After: aggregate positioning IS the most informed market signal. This is a discontinuous jump in signal quality — the S-curve's inflection point.

> **On naming.** This is a **matching-density amplification regime**, not a formal phase transition. Density-threshold formalization (a `N · IMR > N_crit` theorem) is queued pending the empirical anchor program (see [[PHENOTYPE--t42-verification]]). "Inflection" is honest; "phase transition" would overclaim.

**The Waterfall (T46) — sequential unlock:**

Trust compounding does not activate all at once. The layers unlock in sequence:

```
Layer 1: e × X          → trust-flow per period (linear, individual)
Layer 2: reinvestment    → output feeds back as input (compounds)
Layer 3: e(X)           → edge itself grows with stock (accelerates)
Layer 4: ΔC(X)          → confidence grows with stock (amplifies)
Layer 5: e^X            → all layers active simultaneously (exponential)
```

Each layer depends on the prior layer being functional. You cannot skip to e^X without first establishing e×X, then reinvestment, then endogenous e, then endogenous ΔC. The waterfall is the operational roadmap — where Darwinex is on this sequence determines what to build next.

**Connection to the fixed point (§8):** The S-curve converges to X* — the state where applying the trust chain reproduces itself. The waterfall describes HOW you get there. The fixed point describes WHAT you converge to.
