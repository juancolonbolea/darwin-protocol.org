## HUMAN_SECTION

The risk engine is what makes DARWINs investable. It takes whatever a trader does — any asset, any leverage, any style — and transforms it into a product with predictable, standardised risk (6.5% monthly VaR, equivalent to holding the S&P 500).

It does this through four steps: (1) normalise the trader's leverage using EURUSD as the universal yardstick (D-Leverage), (2) adjust specific positions that exceed risk boundaries (generating the engine's own trades if needed), (3) scale the whole portfolio so the investor gets 6.5% VaR, and (4) calculate how DARWINs in a portfolio diversify each other.

The engine deliberately gives traders a discretion zone (3.2%–6.4% VaR) — if a trader reduces risk intentionally, the engine doesn't fight them by scaling up. But it can scale up if the trader runs too conservatively, keeping the DARWIN investable.

The fact that the engine generates its own trades and breaks the 1:1 correlation between trader and investor is regulatorily significant: it proves Darwinex manufactures a financial product rather than merely copying trades.
