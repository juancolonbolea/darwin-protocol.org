## Ongoing research areas

The DARWIN Economy generates a unique dataset — 12+ years of verified track records from 13,000+ traders across 70+ countries. This data enables research that is not possible anywhere else:

- **Trader survival analysis** — What distinguishes traders who sustain performance from those who do not? The 100K dataset contains the answer, across every market condition since 2012.

- **Crowd selection dynamics** — How does a transparent meritocracy allocate capital differently from opaque institutional processes? INDX provides a real-time answer.

- **Internal matching efficiency** — As the community grows, what is the relationship between network size and transaction cost reduction? The matching engine produces the data daily.

- **Trust compounding mechanics** — How does verified transparency affect capital flows over time? The DARWIN Economy is a 12-year experiment in open trust.
