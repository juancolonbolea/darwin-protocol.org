## Two tokens. One economy.

The DARWIN Economy has two tokens. They serve different purposes, but they tell the same story: contribution earns reward, and reward becomes ownership.

---

## INDX — What you earn

**INDX is tokenized trust.**

INDX is minted when the community's credibility matches outside confidence. It only exists when BOTH are present — edge without capital or capital without edge produces nothing. INDX is the product of real skill meeting real investment.

### How INDX is earned

Every contribution to the DARWIN Economy that creates measurable value generates INDX. Trade profitably. Coach effectively. Source talented members. Build useful tools. Process data into investable signals. Deploy capital wisely. The protocol measures the contribution. INDX follows.

### Five functions of INDX

| Function | How it works |
|----------|-------------|
| **Reward** | Earned proportional to your contribution. The primary income token. |
| **Stake** | Wagered in Booster challenges. Bet on yourself. |
| **Payment** | Spent on marketplace services — coaches, tools, analytics. |
| **Prize** | Paid to DarwinIA competition winners. |
| **Gateway** | Convertible to DWX — the pathway from earning to owning. |

### What makes INDX different

INDX is not a speculative token. It is not mined. It is not airdropped. It is earned — through verified, measurable contribution to an economy that produces real returns.

When the community succeeds, more INDX is minted. When you improve, your share of that INDX grows. Your earnings compound when the community compounds.

---

## DWX — What you own

**DWX is protocol ownership.**

DWX represents ownership in the DARWIN Protocol itself. While INDX is the currency of daily contribution, DWX is the currency of long-term alignment. Holding DWX means your interests are permanently aligned with the community's success.

### How DWX is acquired

DWX is earned, not purchased. The pathway: contribute to the economy, earn INDX, convert INDX to DWX. There is no shortcut. Ownership is the product of sustained contribution.

### The exchange rate tells the story

The relationship between DWX and INDX evolves as the protocol matures:

| Phase | Exchange Rate | What it means |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| **Early** | DWX >> INDX | Growth premium. Founders' equity appreciates as the economy scales. Early contributors are rewarded for building before the flywheel spun. |
| **Convergence** | DWX approaching INDX | Members convert earned INDX to DWX. Ownership progressively transfers from founders to the community. |
| **Maturity (1:1)** | DWX = INDX | Earning equals owning. The protocol is its members. Merit owns the market. |

### What convergence means

The founding team's exit and the mission's completion are the same event.

Building the protocol to maturity IS the exit. When DWX and INDX converge at 1:1, the economy has achieved what it set out to do: a self-sustaining meritocracy where the people who create the value own the infrastructure that rewards them.

This is not a promise of future returns. It is a description of the mechanism. The exchange rate converges because ownership transfers from builders to contributors as the economy matures. The math is transparent. The trajectory is public.

---

## How the tokens work together

**INDX** is your paycheck. You earn it daily, weekly, monthly — whenever your contribution creates measurable value. You spend it on services, stake it in challenges, use it to pay for your membership.

**DWX** is your equity. You convert INDX to DWX when you want to stop spending and start owning. Your DWX compounds as the protocol grows. It represents your permanent stake in the economy you helped build.

The two tokens create a natural progression:

1. **PLAY** — You pay EUR 45/month. No tokens yet. You are building your track record.
2. **EARN** — You mint your first INDX. You are now a net earner. INDX accumulates.
3. **OWN** — You convert INDX to DWX. You are now an owner. Your stake compounds.

---

## The alignment

Both tokens are designed so that the only way to benefit is to contribute. There is no way to earn INDX without creating value. There is no way to acquire DWX without earning INDX first. And there is no way DWX appreciates unless the community succeeds.

Self-interest and collective interest are the same thing. The tokens enforce it.
