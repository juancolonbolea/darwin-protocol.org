## Same deal for everyone

There is one membership. One price. One set of rules. What you earn depends entirely on what you contribute. No tiers. No VIP. No "platinum" packages. The meritocracy starts at the door.

---

## What you pay

**EUR 45/month.**

High enough that tourists leave. Low enough that no one with genuine ambition is gated. Designed so members with merit break even quickly.

There are no hidden fees. No spread markups. No commission inflation. The membership fee is the membership fee.

---

## What you get

### The rails
Access to the DARWIN Protocol's infrastructure: the risk engine, the matching engine, and the attestation system. Every trade you make is logged, risk-adjusted, and published. Your track record is not a marketing claim — it is proof.

### Your DARWIN
Your public, risk-adjusted, unfalsifiable track record. Published on the exchange. Calculated at wholesale rates, without spread markup or commission inflation. Your Quote measures your skill, not your broker's pricing.

### The exchange listing
Your DARWIN is listed on the DARWIN Exchange, where investors — from individual members to institutional allocators — can allocate capital to the traders the risk engine identifies as skilled. You are immediately visible to existing investor confidence.

### The 100K dataset
Twelve years of verified track records. Over 100,000 DARWINs — winners and losers. This dataset powers the diagnostics, pattern recognition, and peer benchmarks that help you develop your edge. A trader joining today inherits 12 years of accumulated learning.

### Rock-bottom execution
Trade execution priced at the best you would find anywhere. The matching engine's internal matching rate means the community generates its own liquidity — reducing costs for everyone.

### The leaderboard
Where you rank against 13,000+ traders from 70+ countries. The leaderboard is the meritocracy made visible. Your position determines your DarwinIA eligibility, your investor visibility, and your earning potential.

### DarwinIA challenges
Included with membership. Monthly capital allocation competitions where your score qualifies you. No entry fee. No application. Pure merit.

## What you earn

Once confidence is awarded — once investors allocate capital to your DARWIN — you earn from the trust-flow.

### The split

| Recipient | Share | What it funds |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| **Investor** | 80% | Returns to the person who deployed capital |
| **Edge-holder (you)** | 15% | Your reward for creating the edge |
| **Community** | 5% | Reinvested wherever it mints the most trust |

The 20% collected by the exchange is split 75/25 between you and the community. You keep the lion's share. The community's 5% is reinvested into infrastructure, challenges, and dataset improvements — which benefits you again.

### How earnings work

- Investors profit from your DARWIN? You earn 15% of their gains.
- Students you coached improve their Quotes? You earn INDX from their development.
- Traders you sourced succeed? You earn from the edge you introduced.
- Strategies you built from the dataset perform? You earn from the signals you created.

Every earning stream is tied to verified, measurable contribution.

## Break-even

When your INDX earned exceeds your EUR 45/month membership cost, you have crossed the meritocratic gate. You are a Member. Everything after that compounds.

The break-even point depends on your contribution:

- **A trader** breaks even when investor allocations to their DARWIN generate enough performance fees.
- **An educator** breaks even when their students' Quote improvements generate enough INDX.
- **A signal processor** breaks even when their allocation strategies attract enough capital.
- **A sourcer** breaks even when the traders they introduced create enough value.

The path varies. The principle does not: **create more value than you consume, and the economy rewards you.**
