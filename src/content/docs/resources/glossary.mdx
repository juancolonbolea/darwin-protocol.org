## Key terms

A complete reference of terms used throughout the DARWIN Economy. Listed alphabetically.

---

### Booster

An optional challenge where you stake a premium on yourself for amplified capital allocation. Tiers range from EUR 125 (EUR 25K allocated) to EUR 930 (EUR 200K allocated). Beat the benchmark, earn 15% of gains. Miss it, lose the premium. Designed as a confidence filter — the economics only work if you genuinely believe in your edge.

### Catalyst

A member who sources, develops, or grows the community. Catalysts bring in new traders, coach existing members, build tools, or create educational content. Their earnings are tied to the verified impact of their contributions.

### Compound (action)

One of the five member actions. Deploy capital. Back the winners. Invest INDX. Make the trust stock bigger. Every euro deployed grows the community's earning potential.

### Contribute (action)

One of the five member actions. Trade. Submit signals. Build your track record. Produce the raw edge the economy runs on. Your DARWIN is your contribution — attested, scored, and available for investment.

### DARWIN

Your public, risk-adjusted, unfalsifiable track record. Built one trade at a time by the risk engine. The name stands for **D**evelop **A**nd **R**eward **W**ith **IN**d**X**. A DARWIN cannot be edited, deleted, or fabricated. It is the universal proof of trading skill in the DARWIN Economy.

### DarwinIA

Monthly capital allocation competition included with every membership. Two tiers: Silver (EUR 30K-200K, 3 months, qualification by rating) and Gold (up to EUR 500K, 6 months, qualification by rank). No entry fee. Your score qualifies you. Pure merit.

### Develop (action)

One of the five member actions. Coach, teach, build tools. Make other members better. Your income is measured by your students' verified results — Quote improvements tracked by the risk engine, not self-reported testimonials.

### DWX

Ownership in the protocol itself. Earned, not purchased. Acquired by converting INDX. The DWX/INDX exchange rate tells the story of the protocol's maturity: early (DWX >> INDX), convergence (DWX approaching INDX), maturity (1:1, earning equals owning). DWX holders are permanently aligned with the community's success.

### Edge (e)

Risk-adjusted performance. Your skill, measured by the risk engine. Edge is the numerator in the earnings equation: your earnings = e (your edge) multiplied by X (the trust stock). Improve your edge, and your share of the trust stock grows.

### Free Trust-Flow (FTF)

The point at which the protocol generates more trust-flow than it costs to operate. FTF > 1 means the economy is self-sustaining. Growth becomes endogenous — the community funds its own expansion without external capital injection.

### INDX

The community's collective performance metric. A composite of the best-performing DARWINs, selected by the protocol. When INDX beats the market, trust is being minted. INDX serves five functions: reward (earned for contribution), stake (wagered in challenges), payment (spent on marketplace services), prize (paid to competition winners), and gateway (convertible to DWX).

### Internal Matching Rate (IMR)

The percentage of trades matched within the community rather than routed to external markets. Higher IMR means lower transaction costs for everyone. The matching engine pools all flow, nets internally, and only overflows to the external market what cannot be matched inside.

### Matching Engine

The system that pools all trading flow within the community. When one member buys and another sells the same instrument, the trades are matched internally — saving the spread and external routing costs. The surplus generated is reinvested into the community.

### Meritocratic Gate

The moment you go from PLAY to EARN. First INDX minted or earned. The point at which the economy recognizes that you create more value than you consume. There is no committee. There is no application. The math decides. *"Until all it takes is merit."*

### Process (action)

One of the five member actions. Model the data. Analyse the 100K dataset. Build allocation strategies. Turn raw edge into investable signals. Signal processors work with 12+ years of verified track records to identify patterns and improve community selection.

### Quote

The universal currency of merit in the DARWIN Economy. Your DARWIN Quote is produced by the risk engine and measures one thing: can you trade? It is risk-adjusted, normalised to 6.5% monthly VaR, and comparable across every trader in the economy regardless of their leverage, market, or strategy.

### Risk Engine

The independent system that normalises all track records to the same risk scale (6.5% monthly VaR). It logs every trade, adjusts for risk, and produces the Quote. The risk engine is the referee — you cannot influence it, negotiate with it, or appeal to it.

### Signal Processor

A member who processes the 100K dataset to generate investable signals and allocation strategies. Signal processors analyse patterns across 12+ years of verified track records and build portfolios from the community's collective edge.

### Signal Provider

A member who contributes edge by trading. Your trades feed the dataset, your DARWIN is available for investment, and your performance contributes to INDX.

### Source (action)

One of the five member actions. Bring new edge into the economy. Recruit talented traders, find untapped markets, open new geographies. When a trader you sourced builds a successful DARWIN, you earn from the edge you introduced.

### The 100K Dataset

Twelve years of verified track records. Over 100,000 DARWINs — winners AND losers. The compound learning curve of the entire community. Powers diagnostics, pattern recognition, peer benchmarks, and educator curricula. A trader joining today inherits the accumulated learning of every trader who came before.

### Trust Stock (X)

The community's accumulated capital and credibility. The denominator in the growth equation. When the trust stock grows — through capital inflows, new members, and reinvested surplus — everyone's earning potential grows. Your earnings = e (your edge) multiplied by X (the trust stock).
