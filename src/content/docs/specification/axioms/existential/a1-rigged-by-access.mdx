title: "A1 — Rigged by Access"

## Statement

Allocation of capital, work, and ownership is decided less by contribution than by access. Trading is the first instance; labor and capital markets follow the same pattern.

Access is information asymmetry made structural. Whoever has better data, faster infrastructure, or privileged order flow allocates more — not whoever has produced the most value. "Access, not merit" is another way of saying "information advantage, not contribution."

## Grounded in

P0 (Information Asymmetry) + P6 (Merit). A1 is literally the two primitives held next to each other: P0 describes what *is* — information is asymmetric, and access to it is unevenly distributed. P6 is its normative inverse — contribution *should* determine reward. A1 names the gap between the two.

## Circle

The outer void. A1 has no circle of its own — e (credibility), ★ (matching), and X (confidence) all exist to close it. It is the condition the other fifteen axioms respond to, not a mechanism among them.

## Why it is an axiom

A1 cannot be derived from anything more basic in this framework because it *is* the framework's starting condition — the observation that motivates every subsequent primitive and axiom. There is no combination of P1–P6 that produces "markets are rigged by access" as a conclusion, because P0 (the asymmetry) is already primitive. A1 is P0 read at the level of capital markets, with P6 supplying the standard against which "rigged" is judged. Removing A1 does not simplify the system — it removes the reason the system exists.

## What it explains

A1 grounds T1 (the flywheel self-accelerates) together with A2, A4, A5, and A7 — the flywheel only has work to do because A1 describes a real, exploitable gap. It also anchors the Conservation force: A1's *negation* — access ceasing to determine allocation — is one half of what Conservation (C) protects, alongside P6 itself. A1 reasserting itself *inside* the protocol (incumbents becoming the new access barrier) is the failure mode named in F5 (Stagnation).

## Force mapping

A1 powers **V (Variation)**, alongside A2, A3, and A13 — it is part of the condition-setting group that creates room for edge to exist and be seen. A1's negation additionally powers **C (Conservation)**: preserving "not rigged by access" is the same claim as preserving the fitness function itself. Without V, there is nothing to select from (F5).

## Economic precedent

A1 has no direct Nobel-cross-check in the Constitution — it is the pre-market condition the certification (A2), screening (A3), and transparency (A13) mechanisms exist to resolve. Those three carry the named precedents (Akerlof, Spence, Rothschild-Stiglitz); A1 is the diagnosis they are the treatment for.
