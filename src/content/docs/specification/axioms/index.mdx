An axiom here is a claim the Constitution accepts without proof and cannot derive from any other axiom or from a prior theorem. Each is grounded in one or more of the seven primitives (P0–P6). Theorems build on axioms; axioms do not build on each other. Independence is not a formality — it is checked. If an axiom's content turns out to be a consequence of another axiom plus existing theorems, it is demoted and its number is marked absorbed. Three numbers in this list carry that mark: A6, A11, A12.

Sixteen numbers have been used. Thirteen are active. The gaps are load-bearing, not gaps to be filled — they are the record of the Constitution correcting itself.

## Existential vs. structural

The axioms split into two groups by what question they answer.

**Existential axioms (A1–A3)** answer *why the system exists*. They describe the world without the protocol: capital and reward are allocated by access rather than contribution, and the two things that would fix this — certifying merit, offering the same terms to everyone — are both achievable.

**Structural axioms (A4–A16)** answer *how the system works*. They describe the machinery once it exists: how matching removes friction, how surplus compounds, how standardisation makes capital allocation rational, how the venue is constrained against extracting from its own members, and how the ★-center pump conserves intent and commits atomically.

## Force-axiom mapping

The existential/structural split above is the *logical* view — why an axiom exists and what it does. There is an orthogonal *temporal* view: which of the four evolutionary forces (Variation, Selection, Inheritance, Propagation), plus Conservation, each axiom powers.

| Force | Axioms | Governs | Without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V** — Variation | A1, A2, A3, A13 | Create the conditions for edge to exist: open access, certify merit, self-select, publish. | F5 — Stagnation: nothing to select from |
| **S** — Selection | A4, A8, A9, A14, A15, A16 | Let merit determine allocation: match, standardise, protect intent, invert power, conserve intent through the pump, commit atomically. | F4 — ★-Collapse: variation without direction |
| **I** — Inheritance | A5, T56 | Compound trust across cycles: reinvest surplus, and — via the theorem T56 — let trust beget edge through the aggregated dataset. | F1 — α-Collapse: each cycle starts at zero |
| **P** — Propagation | A7, A10 | Attract confidence from outside: proof compounds into capital. | F2 — β-Collapse: an internal ceiling |
| **C** — Conservation | A1 (negation), P6 | Preserve the fitness function: merit, not extraction, determines outcomes. | Extraction — compounding serves the wrong people |

Both views coexist. Nothing here is metaphorical: the same sixteen numbers are simply read two ways — by what they claim (existential/structural) and by what they power (V/S/I/P/C).
