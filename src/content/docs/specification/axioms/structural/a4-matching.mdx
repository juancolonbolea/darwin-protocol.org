## Statement

Any pairing that removes friction creates surplus. Five dimensions: flow, knowledge, capital, community, tool. Matching is a by-product of community activity (P4), not the goal itself. The friction it eliminates is the cost of P0 — information asymmetry made tangible as spread, search cost, and mismatch.

## Grounded in

P0 (Information Asymmetry) + P4 (Matching). P4 supplies the mechanism; P0 supplies what the mechanism is removing.

## Circle

★ — the community/matching circle. A4 is the operational definition of what happens inside it.

## Why it is an axiom

A4's claim — that friction removal *creates* surplus, rather than merely relocating it — is not derivable from P0 or P4 individually. P0 defines the cost; P4 defines the process; A4 is the assertion that running the process against the cost produces a net gain rather than a wash. This is an empirical-structural claim about matching markets, not a logical consequence of the primitives' definitions. It has to be posited.

## What it explains

A4 is one of five grounding axioms for T1 (the flywheel self-accelerates), and directly grounds T2 (internal matching rate rises with N), T18 (the 98% are the filtering mechanism), T26 (collective edge capacity exceeds the sum of individual capacity), T51 (Non-Rivalry Duality — the physical explanation for $e^X$), and T55 (Provision Commons — Ostrom). It also grounds [A8](/specification/axioms/structural/a8-standardisation/), [A9](/specification/axioms/structural/a9-no-extraction/), and [A15](/specification/axioms/structural/a15-intent-conservation/), each of which depends on matching having already established friction removal as a source of surplus.

## Force mapping

A4 powers **S (Selection)**, alongside A8, A9, and A14 — the group of axioms that let merit determine allocation once variation exists. Without it, the system has edge to select from but no mechanism to act on the selection: F4 (★-Collapse) — variation without direction.
