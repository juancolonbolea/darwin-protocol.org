## Statement

Matching over the five dimensions removes P0 friction at a cost below the friction removed, up to a per-dimension capacity beyond which marginal pairings are non-positive. The dimensions are separable.

## v13 note

The statement now carries only what the primitives do not: that friction removal is net-positive, that each dimension has a capacity beyond which marginal pairings stop paying, and that the dimensions are separable. The clauses that merely restated P0 and P4 are dropped. The scaling-in-N claim moves to a density lemma that T2, T26 and T29 cite directly, rather than living inside the axiom. Source: the axiom stress test, `analysis/axiom-stress/SYNTHESIS.md` in the repository.

## Grounded in

P0 (Information Asymmetry) + P4 (Matching). P0 supplies the friction being removed; P4 supplies the process that removes it. A4 adds what neither contains: that the removal is net-positive up to a per-dimension capacity, and that the dimensions can be treated separately.

## Circle

★ — the community/matching circle. A4 is the operational definition of what happens inside it.

## Why it is an axiom

A4's claim — that friction removal *creates* surplus, rather than merely relocating it — is not derivable from P0 or P4 individually. P0 defines the cost; P4 defines the process; A4 is the assertion that running the process against the cost produces a net gain rather than a wash. This is an empirical-structural claim about matching markets, not a logical consequence of the primitives' definitions. It has to be posited.

## What it explains

A4 is one of five grounding axioms for T1 (the flywheel self-accelerates), and directly grounds T2 (internal matching rate rises with N), T18 (the 98% are the filtering mechanism), T26 (collective edge capacity exceeds the sum of individual capacity), T51 (Non-Rivalry Duality — the physical explanation for $e^X$), and T55 (Provision Commons — Ostrom). Together with [A9](/specification/axioms/structural/a9-no-extraction/) it also yields the netting and anti-differencing requirements carried by the matching and settlement components.

## Force mapping

A4 powers **S (Selection)**, alongside A8 and A14 — the group of axioms that let merit determine allocation once variation exists. Without it, the system has edge to select from but no mechanism to act on the selection: F4 (★-Collapse) — variation without direction.
