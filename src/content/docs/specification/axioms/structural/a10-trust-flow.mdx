## Statement

## Why it is an axiom

A10 could look like a restatement of P2's definition (confidence as a forward-looking bet on P1). It is not: P2 defines confidence as a primitive relationship between capital and certified credibility in general. A10 makes the separate claim that trust-flow specifically — the deployment of *accumulated trust stock*, not merely capital — is itself an act of confidence-loaded allocation, and that this act is what "trust-flow" means operationally. That trust *behaves* this way, rather than sitting inert once accrued, is a structural claim about the system's dynamics that has to be posited rather than derived from P2 or P3 alone.
