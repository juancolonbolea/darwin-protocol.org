**Status: demoted in v13.**

Trust-flow is vocabulary, not an axiom. The term is defined in the protocol glossary; the reinvestment claim it used to carry — that accrued trust is redeployed rather than left inert — is [A5 (Retention)](/specification/axioms/structural/a5-surplus-compounds/). The Propagation force is powered by [A7](/specification/axioms/structural/a7-double-recurrence/) plus T9, the same shape as Inheritance being A5 plus T56.

## Statement (v12)

Trust-flow is confidence-loaded allocation: allocating trust stock (P3) with the confidence that it will yield further trust at a rate at least equal to $e$. This is not a measurement taken after the fact — it is the act of allocation itself. Grounded in P3 (Trust) + P2 (Confidence).

## Why it was demoted

The axiom stress test found A10 to be a definition of a term plus a claim A5 already makes. What A10 asserted about the *act* of allocation is the operational content of "trust-flow" as a word; what it asserted about redeployment is A5's retention claim under another name. Neither half is an independent proposition, and no theorem needs A10 once T9 is sourced from A5.

One residue was identified and deliberately not promoted: *accrued trust stock is redeployable as allocative capital*. It is irreducible and currently unstated, but no proof requires it once the Proof 9 and T35 typing is repaired — the proofs consume A5's realised quantity, not A10's believed lower bound. It is revisited only if a derivation then fails.
