## Statement

Trust → confidence, via proof and INDX: the β-loop. Individual Quotes aggregate into INDX; outsiders observe that collective proof and award capital ($\Delta C$). This is the external half of the trust differential equation — the irreducible behavioural assumption that proven, comparable, transparent edge rationally attracts capital. It absorbs the former [A6](/specification/axioms/structural/a6-collective-credibility/).

β has a precondition: $\beta = 0$ without standardisation ([A8](/specification/axioms/structural/a8-standardisation/)). Outsiders cannot rationally allocate to incomparable instruments — the risk engine is what makes the β-loop physically possible.

The complementary trust→edge loop (α) is **no longer axiomatic**. Aggregate-level α is proven as T56 (Trust-to-Edge Aggregation), a specialisation of T47's resolution of the aggregate-α gap. Only β remains an axiom.

## Grounded in

P3 (Trust) + P1 (Credibility) + P2 (Confidence) + A8 (Standardisation). The dependency on A8 is direct and stated: without a comparison surface, there is nothing for confidence to rationally attach to.

## Circle

e∩X→X (β) — the overlap of credibility and confidence, projecting outward into X.

## Why it is an axiom

That proven, comparable, transparent edge rationally attracts outside capital is a behavioural claim about how capital allocators respond to public proof — it is not derivable from the primitives' definitions, and it is not provable from A2 (certification) plus A8 (standardisation) alone, because those establish that a comparable proof *exists*, not that capital *responds* to it. A7 is the irreducible assumption that closes that gap. This is why the Constitution treats A7 as split: the α half (trust compounding into more edge via the dataset) turned out to be derivable — T56 — but the β half (capital responding to proof) does not reduce to anything more basic and stays axiomatic.

## What it explains

A7 is one of five grounding axioms for T1 (self-accelerating flywheel), and grounds T6 (the dataset as trust→edge vehicle), T7 ($e$ = platform effect, $X$ = network effect), T8 (three panels feed X), T10 (the $e$ inflection), T11 (transparency turns selfishness into social trust), T19 (deadwood shareholders), T31 (certification externality), T33 (nested meritocracy), T37 (self-reinforcing ownership transfer), T42 (external validation), and T61 (DWX decay). It is a direct input to T47/T56, the theorem pair that carries the α-loop the axiom itself no longer needs to.

## Force mapping

A7 (β-only) powers **P (Propagation)**, alongside A10 — trust attracting confidence from outside is definitionally propagation. Without it, edge accumulates that nobody funds: F2 (β-Collapse).
