**Status: derivable from A11+A5 (A11 itself absorbed into A5).**

## What it said

A12 claimed that the three-party split is a confidence allocation — the specific ratio (75/25) by which reward divides between the parties involved was treated as a structural claim in its own right. Demoted 6 April 2026.

## Why it was absorbed

Once the three-vector reinvestment mechanism (formerly A11, now inside A5) is established, the *ratio* at which surplus splits across recipients is a choice made within that mechanism, not a separate law of nature. A structural axiom claims something must be true of any correctly-built system; a specific split percentage is a governance decision that could in principle be set differently without breaking anything else the Constitution proves. Treating 75/25 as axiomatic overstated what the number actually is. This closed a tracked open gap (G12).

## Where its content lives now

The split ratio now lives as a governance surface — see T21 (the allocation decision as a governance surface) and T59 (continuous irreplaceability), which formalises the 75% figure as a *reference point* of a continuous function of originality, $\psi(O)$, rather than a flat tier. [A5 — Surplus Compounds](/specification/axioms/structural/a5-surplus-compounds/) carries the reinvestment mechanism the split operates within; [A9 — No Extraction](/specification/axioms/structural/a9-no-extraction/) is the constraint on where the split's outputs are allowed to go.

The number A12 is retired for this content and is not reused.
