## Statement

Surplus — rescued information-asymmetry cost — is reinvested wherever it mints the most trust, which produces more trust, which produces more surplus. When collective edge $e > 0$, each cycle produces more surplus than it consumes.

Three reinvestment vectors carry that surplus: **Compound** (organic growth at rate e), **Source** (new members → matching density), **Develop** (improve edge rate and rails). This three-vector decomposition absorbs the former A11 — it is A5's mechanism, not an independent claim.

## Grounded in

P0 (Information Asymmetry) + P3 (Trust) + P2 (Confidence). P0 is what generates the surplus in the first place (rescued asymmetry cost); P3 and P2 are what the surplus is reinvested *into*.

## Circle

★→e,X — surplus flows out of the community circle into both credibility and confidence.

## Why it is an axiom

That surplus *compounds* — rather than simply accumulates linearly — cannot be derived from A4 (matching creates surplus) alone. A4 establishes that a surplus exists; A5 makes the separate claim that reinvesting it produces more of the mechanism that generated it, closing a loop rather than a one-off transfer. This loop-closing claim, plus the specific allocation rule ("wherever it mints the most trust"), is a distinct structural commitment that has to be posited independently.

## What it explains

A5 is one of five grounding axioms for T1 (self-accelerating flywheel) and directly grounds T9 (trust-flow is the reinvestment act, with A10), T15/T16 (front-loading and the prescribed S-curve), T17 ("mints the most trust" formalised as targeting the P0 vector closest to inflection), T21 (allocation as governance surface), T25 (contribution scoring), T28 (Linear-Network Decomposition), and T46 (the Waterfall — asset-class cascade). Together with T56, A5 powers the Inheritance force.

## Force mapping

A5 powers **I (Inheritance)** together with T56 — the theorem that derives the aggregate trust-to-edge loop A5's reinvestment makes possible. Without A5, each cycle starts at zero: F1 (α-Collapse).
