## Statement

Rescued surplus is retained and redeployed into trust-minting capacity, not distributed or held. Where marginal trust return ≥ 1 and e > 0, each cycle yields more than it consumes. Allocation across vectors is governance.

## v13 note

Retention is the axiom — the directive complement of A9's prohibition: surplus is redeployed into trust-minting capacity rather than rebated or left idle. The three vectors (Compound, Source, Develop) become definition rather than axiom content, and the "wherever it mints the most trust" argmax becomes a governance choice under T21. A companion failure theorem states the consequence of the negation: surplus distributed or held flattens the curve to linear. Source: the axiom stress test, `analysis/axiom-stress/SYNTHESIS.md` in the repository.

## Grounded in

P3 (Trust) + A4. P3 is the stock that retained surplus is redeployed into; A4 is what produces the surplus in the first place. The retention claim is what neither supplies: that the surplus is neither distributed nor held.

## Circle

★→e,X — surplus flows out of the community circle into both credibility and confidence.

## Why it is an axiom

That surplus *compounds* — rather than simply accumulates linearly — cannot be derived from A4 (matching creates surplus) alone. A4 establishes that a surplus exists; A5 makes the separate claim that reinvesting it produces more of the mechanism that generated it, closing a loop rather than a one-off transfer. This loop-closing claim — retention rather than distribution — is a distinct structural commitment that has to be posited independently; where the retained surplus is then pointed is governance, not axiom.

## What it explains

A5 is one of five grounding axioms for T1 (self-accelerating flywheel) and directly grounds T9 (trust-flow is the reinvestment act), T15/T16 (front-loading and the prescribed S-curve), T17 ("mints the most trust" formalised as targeting the P0 vector closest to inflection), T21 (allocation as governance surface), T25 (contribution scoring), T28 (Linear-Network Decomposition), and T46 (the Waterfall — asset-class cascade). Together with T56, A5 powers the Inheritance force.

## Force mapping

A5 powers **I (Inheritance)** together with T56 — the theorem that derives the aggregate trust-to-edge loop A5's reinvestment makes possible. Without A5, each cycle starts at zero: F1 (α-Collapse).
