**Status: absorbed into [A7](/specification/axioms/structural/a7-double-recurrence/).**

## What it said

A6 claimed that collective credibility attracts confidence: individual Quotes aggregate into a visible track record, outsiders observe that aggregate, and capital follows. Grounded in P1 (Credibility) + P2 (Confidence).

## Why it was absorbed

A6's content is exactly A7's β-channel. A7 states that trust begets confidence via proof — and the mechanism by which that happens is precisely "individual Quotes aggregate into INDX; outsiders observe collective proof and award capital." There was no residual claim in A6 once A7 was stated with its mechanism made explicit. An axiom that restates another axiom's mechanism under a different number is not independent, so it was demoted in the v7 audit hardening pass (11 Apr 2026).

## Where its content lives now

Read [A7 — Double Recurrence](/specification/axioms/structural/a7-double-recurrence/). What A6 called "collective credibility attracts confidence" is A7's β-loop: individual Quotes aggregate → outsiders observe → confidence (ΔC) is awarded. A7's own text names this explicitly as the absorbed mechanism.

The number A6 is retired for this content and is not reused.
