## Statement

No party, the operator included, may take the other side of member flow or trade on aggregate flow information.

## v13 note

The statement is now the prohibition alone, and it binds the operator explicitly. The CCP mechanics move to the component layer as a conformance requirement, and the "exactly two destinations" budget clause becomes a governance choice under T21. A9 is also re-assigned from Selection to Conservation: a prohibition on extraction preserves the fitness function rather than performing selection. Source: the axiom stress test, `analysis/axiom-stress/SYNTHESIS.md` in the repository.

## Grounded in

P4 (Matching) + P6 (Merit). P4 supplies the venue mechanics the prohibition binds; P6 supplies the reason extraction inside it is disallowed — extraction is the thing merit-based allocation exists to prevent.

## Circle

★ constraint — A9 is not a circle itself but the rule governing what may and may not happen inside the community circle.

## Why it is an axiom

A9 cannot be derived from A4 (matching creates surplus): A4 says friction removal creates surplus, but says nothing about who may stand on the other side of the flow that produces it. A9 is the separate, structural commitment that no party — the operator included — takes that side or trades on what the aggregate flow reveals. This is a constitutional choice about the venue's own incentives, binding against its own governors, not a consequence of matching mechanics.

## What it explains

A9 grounds T12 (power inversion organizes individuals off the menu, with A4+A14), T14 (competition between market makers is a bug in mutualized systems), T19/T20 (deadwood shareholders and closing all three extraction levels), T22 (mutualized monopoly aligned with endgame), T34 (trust ownership), T41 (the Challenge Marketplace), T54 (Two-Step Resolution — Holmström), T55 (Provision Commons — Ostrom), T59 (continuous irreplaceability), and T61 (DWX decay). It is [A8](/specification/axioms/structural/a8-standardisation/)'s credibility precondition: if A9 fails, the standardiser is biased, A8 loses trust, and β collapses to zero.

## Force mapping

A9 powers **C (Conservation)**, alongside A1's negation and P6 — a prohibition on extraction preserves the fitness function rather than performing selection. Without it, surplus leaks to intermediaries instead of being recycled and the system degrades to a traditional broker.

## Economic precedent

A9 is a joint grounding axiom for T54 (Two-Step Resolution — Holmström, 2016): together with A8, it makes trader actions fully observable, converting a moral-hazard problem into a measurable adverse-selection one. It also grounds T55 (Provision Commons — Ostrom, 2009): A9's automated, continuous monitoring exceeds Ostrom's own monitoring design principle for sustainable commons governance.
