**Status: demoted in v13.**

Netting and its mandatoriness are double-entry bookkeeping from [A9](/specification/axioms/structural/a9-no-extraction/) plus [A4](/specification/axioms/structural/a4-matching/), not a conservation law. Composition-infeasibility is a cohort-size and anti-differencing requirement. Both are conformance requirements of the matching and settlement components, and they live in the component specification rather than in the axiom set.

## Statement (v12)

The ★-center pump preserves aggregate risk-adjusted directional intent across its transforms — normalise, match, size, abstract, route, certify. Individual identity is destroyed through netting: aggregation gives cryptographic-equivalent privacy, and composition (recovering an individual signal from the aggregate) is infeasible. Aggregate intent — the vector sum of every member's risk-adjusted signal — is the conserved invariant that external markets certify. Grounded in P4 (Matching) + A9 (No Extraction).

## Why it was demoted

Two of the three claims dissolve on inspection. That the netted quantity balances is double-entry bookkeeping: A4 establishes that matching removes friction, A9 establishes that nothing inside the venue skims the result, and balanced journaling follows from the two without a separate invariant. The mandatoriness of netting follows the same way.

The third claim — cryptographic-equivalent privacy through aggregation — is refuted by the pump's own design: if aggregation alone were cryptographic-equivalent, the ε-noise layer would be redundant, and it is not. What survives is a real engineering requirement on cohort size and anti-differencing, stated where it can be tested and conformed to.

Full reasoning: `analysis/axiom-stress/SYNTHESIS.md` in the repository.
