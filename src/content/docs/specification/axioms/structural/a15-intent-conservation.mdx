## Statement

The ★-center pump preserves aggregate risk-adjusted directional intent across its transforms — normalise, match, size, abstract, route, certify. Individual identity is destroyed through netting: aggregation gives cryptographic-equivalent privacy, and composition (recovering an individual signal from the aggregate) is infeasible. Aggregate intent — the vector sum of every member's risk-adjusted signal — is the conserved invariant that external markets certify.

A15 is posited as a conservation law, not derived from the merit axioms — deriving it from them would be circular, since the merit axioms describe *what should happen to* intent, not that a quantity of it is conserved through processing. The closest analogy is energy conservation in physics: a structural invariant of the mechanism, not a consequence of the mechanism's purpose.

Several consequences follow once A15 is posited: netting becomes mandatory (there is nothing else the invariant could be settled through); external venues are subcontracted crystallisers of the netted signal, not competitors to it; composition-infeasibility is structural rather than a privacy feature layered on afterward; and double-entry INDX accounting is well-defined, because a conserved quantity is what permits balanced journaling in the first place.

## Grounded in

P4 (Matching) + A9 (No Extraction). P4 supplies the pump's mechanics; A9 supplies the guarantee that nothing inside the pump siphons off intent for private use, which is what makes conservation — rather than leakage — the correct description of what the pump does to it.

## Circle

★ pump — the internal mechanics of the community circle, one level more granular than A4's general matching claim.

## Why it is an axiom

A15 cannot be derived from A4 (matching eliminates friction) or A9 (no extraction) individually or together. A4 establishes that matching creates surplus; A9 establishes that the venue does not skim that surplus. Neither says anything about whether the *quantity being matched* — aggregate intent — is itself conserved through the pump's sequence of transforms, or whether some of it is lost, distorted, or created in processing. That the vector sum survives normalise→match→size→abstract→route→certify intact is an independent structural claim about the pump's internal physics, and — like a genuine conservation law — it has to be posited rather than proven from purpose-level claims about what the system is *for*.

## What it explains

A15, together with T5 ($e^X$ is algebraically literal) and T42 (External Validation), grounds T64 (the Patience Lever — internal match rate is increasing in cycle length, up to the point where delay cost exceeds marginal matching surplus). A15 is also the derivation root of the Stock-Flow Conservation named principle: because a conserved quantity exists, the INDX ledger can be kept triple-entry (mint / loss-destroy / cashout-destroy) rather than merely additive.

## Force mapping

A15 is one of the newest axioms in the Constitution (added v12) and is not listed in the earlier prose Force-Axiom Mapping table, but the formal dependency graph places it under **S (Selection)**, alongside A4, A8, A9, and A16 — it is part of the machinery that lets merit determine allocation cleanly, specifically by guaranteeing the allocation-relevant quantity (aggregate intent) is not lost or corrupted in the process. Without it, the pump's output would not be trustworthy as a certification target, and downstream selection would be selecting on a distorted signal.
