## Statement

Certified results are public and un-gated; strategy IP is encrypted; the live process is private. The boundary is set at a granularity and lag that preserves the capacity of the edge disclosed.

## v13 note

The statement now names the boundary *and* its calibration: granularity and lag are set so that publishing an edge does not destroy the capacity of that edge. The closing "individual selfishness converts into social trust" sentence is cut — that is T11 verbatim, not axiom content. The stress test's initial derivability verdict was overturned on verify: A2's "publicly attested" does not entail un-gated access, and A3 presupposes IP encryption rather than establishing it. Source: the axiom stress test, `analysis/axiom-stress/SYNTHESIS.md` in the repository.

## Grounded in

P0 (its data dimension) + A2 + A3. P0's data dimension is what the boundary partitions; A2 supplies the certified result that gets published; A3 presupposes the IP encryption that the boundary makes explicit.

## Circle

e mechanism — A13 is the operating rule of the credibility circle, not a separate circle.

## Why it is an axiom

A13 asserts a specific, non-obvious design choice — publish results while encrypting the method that produced them, at a granularity and lag that leaves the disclosed edge still worth having. That claim does not follow from A2 alone: A2 establishes that contribution can be estimated and attested, but attestation does not entail un-gated access, and it says nothing about what must stay encrypted or at what lag. Nor does it follow from A3, which presupposes IP encryption rather than establishing it. A13 is a distinct structural commitment about *which* information the inversion exposes, and at what granularity.

## Force mapping

A13 powers **V (Variation)**, alongside A1, A2, and A3 — publication is what makes newly-produced edge visible enough to be selected on. Without it: F5 (Stagnation).

## Economic precedent

A13 directly resolves **Akerlof's adverse selection** (2001): the DARWIN Quote is a *direct* quality signal, not a proxy like education in Spence's model, which eliminates market-for-lemons dynamics outright rather than merely mitigating them. It is named explicitly in the Constitution as the mechanism that resolves this.
