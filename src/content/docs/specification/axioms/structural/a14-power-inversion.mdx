## Statement

Members hold governance standing and the residual claim on the venue; obligations to members are not contingent on external power.

## v13 note

The statement is now the standing claim: governance standing plus the residual claim, and obligations that do not bend to external power. "At the table, not on the menu" is demoted to a theorem of A4, A9 and netting — it follows from the machinery rather than needing to be posited. What is left is the irreducible half, and it is the sole support of F8. Source: the axiom stress test, `analysis/axiom-stress/SYNTHESIS.md` in the repository.

## Grounded in

P6 (Merit) + A3. P6 supplies the mandate that those who contribute hold the residual claim rather than a counterparty relationship to it; A3 supplies the uniform-terms venue that standing is held in.

## Circle

★ mechanism — A14 is the governance rule of the community circle, complementing A9's prohibition on the same circle.

## Why it is an axiom

A14 could look derivable from A9 (no counterparty extraction): if the venue may not take the other side, members are protected as a side effect. But A9 is a *prohibition* — it says what no party may do. A14 is a *conferral*: members hold governance standing and the residual claim, and what the venue owes them does not bend to external power. A venue could satisfy A9 in full and still hold its members as customers with no standing and no claim on the residual. A14 rules that out independently — it is a claim about who the venue belongs to, not about what may be taken from it.

## What it explains

A14 grounds T12 (power inversion organizes individuals off the menu, with A4+A9), T13 (both inversions together create compounding trust), T20 (three extraction levels must all close), T32 (regulatory architecture), and T43 (two-way certification, with A2+A3+T34). It is [A8](/specification/axioms/structural/a8-standardisation/)'s sibling axiom under Selection: standardisation resolves the credibility side of the crossing; power inversion resolves the intent side.

## Force mapping

A14 powers **S (Selection)**, alongside A4 and A8 — standing is part of what lets merit determine allocation cleanly. Without it: F4 (★-Collapse) — the venue loses structural protection and surplus leaks to intermediaries.

## Economic precedent

A14 directly resolves **Holmström's moral hazard** (2016): full observability via the risk engine — every position, every leverage change passes through the matching engine — satisfies Holmström's informativeness principle, leaving no hidden actions possible. This is named explicitly in the Constitution as the axiom's resolution mechanism.
