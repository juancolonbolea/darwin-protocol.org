## Statement

Individuals sit at the table, not on the menu. The venue *is* the union.

A14 is the intent inversion rendered as a governance rule — the generative method applied to intent asymmetry: convention (individual intent exploited by privileged counterparties) → inversion (intent protected by mutualized matching, CCP sole counterparty) → test (does trust emerge? yes — moral hazard resolves). A14 is how the protocol inverts the intent dimension of P0.

## Grounded in

P4 (Matching) + P6 (Merit). P4 supplies the mutualized-matching mechanism that protects intent; P6 supplies the mandate for protecting it rather than exploiting it.

## Circle

★ mechanism — A14 is the governance rule of the community circle, complementing A9's structural constraint on the same circle.

## Why it is an axiom

A14 could look derivable from A9 (no private extraction): if extraction is banned, intent is protected as a side effect. But A9 constrains what the venue does with *surplus*; A14 makes the separate claim about *governance* — that individuals are structurally positioned as participants with standing, not as counterparties whose flow can be read and traded against. A venue could satisfy A9 (no skimming) while still allowing privileged actors to observe and front-run member intent through some other channel. A14 rules that out independently — it is a claim about power, not about extraction.

## What it explains

A14 grounds T12 (power inversion organizes individuals off the menu, with A4+A9), T13 (both inversions together create compounding trust), T20 (three extraction levels must all close), T32 (regulatory architecture), and T43 (two-way certification, with A2+A3+T34). It is [A8](/specification/axioms/structural/a8-standardisation/)'s sibling axiom under Selection: standardisation resolves the credibility side of the crossing; power inversion resolves the intent side.

## Force mapping

A14 powers **S (Selection)**, alongside A4, A8, and A9 — protecting intent is part of what lets merit determine allocation cleanly. Without it: F4 (★-Collapse) — the venue loses structural protection and surplus leaks to intermediaries.

## Economic precedent

A14 directly resolves **Holmström's moral hazard** (2016): full observability via the risk engine — every position, every leverage change passes through the matching engine — satisfies Holmström's informativeness principle, leaving no hidden actions possible. This is named explicitly in the Constitution as the axiom's resolution mechanism.
