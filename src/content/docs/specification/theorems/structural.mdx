This group covers two things: the structural claims the constitution files under "Structural" (T23–T26), and the two network corollaries that immediately follow from them (T27–T28).

---

## Structural (T23–T26)

**T23 — The Knowledge System IS the System.** The protocol's knowledge system — certification, attribution, the dataset — is not a support function bolted onto trading. It is the system: merit ($P6$) applied to certified contribution (A2) is what the protocol actually does.

**Derives from** P6+A2. **Consequence:** investment in certification and attribution infrastructure is not overhead. It is investment in the core product.

---

**T24 — Rails (1:N) / Exchange (M:N) / Method.** The protocol separates into three structural layers: Rails (one-to-many infrastructure serving any member), Exchange (many-to-many matching among members), and Method (the certification and risk logic that makes both work). Each layer scales differently.

**Derives from** A4+T7. **Consequence:** growth, partnership, and regulatory strategy should be evaluated per layer, not for "the protocol" as an undifferentiated whole — the three layers carry different economics.

---

**T25 — Contribution Score = Three-Vector Weighting.** An individual's contribution score is a weighted combination of the three extraction-adjacent vectors identified in T19–T20 — stock, flow, and governance contribution — not a single trading-performance number.

**Derives from** T19+A5+T20. **Consequence:** rewarding contribution fairly requires scoring more than trading edge. Sourcing, developing, and governing well are each separately measurable and separately rewardable.

---

**T26 — Collective Edge Capacity Exceeds Sum of Individual.** The community's collective capacity to generate edge exceeds the simple sum of each member's individual edge, because matching (A4), the density scaling that A4 implies ([L1](/specification/theorems/core-flywheel)), and the effects of scale (T2) create combinations of information no individual member can access alone.

**Derives from** A4+L1+T2+T18. **Consequence:** the case for staying inside the community rather than trading independently is not just cost savings — it is access to a strictly larger edge-generating capacity than any individual can replicate alone.

## Network Corollaries (T27–T28)

**T27 — Protocol Network Corollary.** The exchange is a protocol connecting autonomous community nodes on shared rails. Each node functions as its own sub-prop, and total protocol value scales as the product of matching dimensions across nodes — $\Pi(\text{matching dims}) \times N(\text{nodes})$ — not as a simple sum. This is internet topology, not franchise topology: each level replicates the structure of the level above it.

**Derives from** T24+A4+T2+A13. **Consequence:** expansion should be evaluated as adding nodes to a network (multiplicative) rather than opening franchises (additive) — an existing node's value rises when a new node joins, which is not true of a franchise system.

---

**T28 — Linear-Network Decomposition.** Every participant — trader, manager, investor, tool builder — creates two kinds of value: linear value they keep individually, and network value the venue captures and reinvests on behalf of everyone. The same formula applies to every participant type; only the matching dimension changes.

**Derives from** A4+A5+A13+T7. **Consequence:** compensation design does not need a bespoke formula per participant type. The linear/network split is universal, which is what makes the reward architecture tractable across very different kinds of contribution.

**Proof:** [Proof 6 in the constitution](/specification/proofs/proof-6-linear-network).
