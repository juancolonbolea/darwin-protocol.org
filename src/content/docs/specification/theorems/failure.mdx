The framework diagnoses its own breakdown through two failure classes. **Class A (F1–F5)** is internal force collapse — one failure mode per evolutionary force, self-inflicted. **Class B (F6–F9)** is adversarial or externally induced failure that can occur even under honest operation. Each failure mode is an information pathology: a stage of the create → protect → liberate lifecycle (T50) breaking down.

---

## Class A — Internal Force Collapse (F1–F5)

**F1 — α-Collapse (Dataset Failure).** If aggregate α is broken (the dataset stops transmitting trust into edge, ¬T56), $e$ becomes constant and exogenous, and $e^X$ collapses to $e \times X$: linear rather than exponential growth. The system keeps functioning; it stops compounding.

**Derives from** ¬T56+T5. **Consequence:** diagnosable as $e(t)$ becoming uncorrelated with $X(t)$; the fix is repairing the dataset pipeline ($P5$) and the selection mechanism (T18), not adding capital.

---

**F2 — β-Collapse (Confidence Failure).** If trust stops attracting confidence (¬A7), $X$ stagnates: credibility keeps accumulating but capital stops following it — edge gets developed that nobody funds.

**Derives from** ¬A7+T5. **Consequence:** diagnosable as a rising Quote alongside flat assets under management; the fix runs through external validation (T42) and outreach, not through improving edge further.

---

**F3 — Death Spiral (Negative Edge).** If collective edge turns negative, trust stock erodes, confidence withdraws, and the dataset degrades — both loops reverse simultaneously, since the same double recurrence that makes growth exponential upward makes decline catastrophic downward.

**Derives from** ¬(e>0)+A7+A5. **Consequence:** diagnosable as declining system-level edge alongside falling internal matching and net outflows; the fix is tightening the selection gate (T18) and shedding low-edge segments (T46 run in reverse), not broadening participation.

---

**F4 — ★-Collapse (Venue Failure).** If the venue extracts (¬A9), stops matching (¬A4), or loses structural power protection (¬A14), surplus leaks to intermediaries instead of being recycled, and the protocol degrades into an ordinary broker.

**Derives from** ¬A9+¬A4+¬A14. **Consequence:** diagnosable as matching surplus flowing to company P&L rather than reinvestment, with internal matching flattening despite volume growth; the fix is constitutional enforcement of A9 and restoration of member governance (A14).

---

**F5 — V-Collapse (Stagnation).** If self-selection becomes over-optimised — barriers too high, screening too tight — variation dries up: no new strategies enter, the dataset ossifies, and selection operates on an ever-shrinking pool. The protocol's own incumbents become the access barrier that A1 describes markets reasserting itself from inside.

**Derives from** ¬V+T18+A3. **Consequence:** diagnosable as a declining count of new providers and a rising average age of top Quotes; the fix is lowering entry barriers and expanding into new asset classes (T46), not further tightening the gate.

---

## Class B — Adversarial & External Failure (F6–F9)

**F6 — Dataset Poisoning.** A coordinated group of providers submits crafted signals to bias the aggregate statistics the risk engine learns from; honest providers then see falling allocation despite genuine edge and withdraw.

**Derives from** ¬P5(robustness)+A2+A8. **Consequence:** mitigated by a Byzantine-robust aggregation threshold — robust estimators and provider diversity caps at the A8 layer — diagnosed via signal-quality divergence between cohorts and robustness-check failures.

---

**F7 — Reflexivity (Soros).** As INDX credibility grows, capital follows it, aggregate positions shift market microstructure, and the original signal degrades — the β-loop poisons its own fuel if capital-following velocity outruns what the signal's half-life can absorb.

**Derives from** ¬P5(stationarity)+A7+T42. **Consequence:** mitigated by capacity capping per strategy and explicit half-life disclosure in the Quote, diagnosed via accelerating alpha decay on top-Quote strategies as assets under management rise.

---

**F8 — Regulatory Weaponisation.** Incumbents lobby for compliance infrastructure sized to their own cost base, re-inverting A14 from outside the protocol — the intent-protection built internally is nullified by intent-capture externally, and uniform terms (A3) become infeasible.

**Derives from** ¬A14(ext)+¬A3+T32. **Consequence:** mitigated by the peripheral structural separation T32 specifies, plus multi-jurisdictional optionality, diagnosed via regulatory fixed costs rising faster than incumbent scale.

---

**F9 — Strategy Convergence / Monoculture.** Uniform scoring homogenises what "good" looks like: rational traders converge on the same objective, strategy correlation rises, effective variation collapses toward a single factor, and selection (T18) operates on a degenerate manifold — a back door into F5 through the scoring layer rather than the entry gate.

**Derives from** ¬V(endog)+A8+T18. **Consequence:** mitigated by multi-objective scoring with orthogonal axes and a diversity bonus in attribution, diagnosed via rising pairwise correlation among certified return streams.

## Named Principles

Four corollaries are named for reference and teaching but are not numbered in the theorem count — they are derivable patterns, not independent claims.

**Normalisation Principle.** The pump applies invertible transforms to signals — risk normalisation, volatility adjustment — and never scores members directly. Scoring discriminates the member; normalisation only reshapes the signal. Derived from A9+P6.

**Stock-Flow Conservation.** INDX flow integrates into trust stock across cycles; a cashout exits that integration and a loss-certification reverses a prior increment. The trust ledger is a balanced triple-entry system: mint, loss-destroy, cashout-destroy. Derived from A15+T42 (bilateral)+definitional accounting.

**Capital-vs-Merit Separation.** Capital is compensated at market rate plus bounded alpha; merit is compensated with DWX (exponential platform equity) and INDX (flow credits). The compounding rate matches the contribution type, and neither side has a claim on the other's compensation structure. Derived from P6+T5.

**Take-rate Ceiling (Patagonia Constraint).** Shareholder compounding is bounded by the aggregate net platform take-rate. Economics above that ceiling is super-profit and reinjected into the community through the DWX→INDX reinvestment channels rather than retained. Derived from P6+A9+A13+T61.
