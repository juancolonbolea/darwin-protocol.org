---
title: "Core Flywheel (L1, T1-T4)"
description: "The matching-density lemma and the self-accelerating growth mechanism: why the protocol compounds once collective edge is positive."
---

The flywheel is the load-bearing mechanical claim of the framework. It says that once collective edge is positive, the system does not need external help to keep growing — each cycle produces more surplus than it consumes. T1–T3 state and extend that claim, and the lemma L1 supplies the scaling result T2 needs.

---

**L1 — Matching Density (lemma).** Within each of A4's five matching dimensions, the count of feasible pairings grows super-linearly in the number of participating members $N$ — pairwise opportunity on the order of $N(N-1)/2$ — up to that dimension's capacity ceiling. Because A4's dimensions are separable, per-dimension densities compose multiplicatively rather than substituting for one another.

**Derives from** A4. **Consequence:** two results are used downstream. Internal match rate is increasing in $N$ at fixed composition (T2), and a competitor attempting replication must populate every dimension at once, because an unpopulated dimension contributes a zero factor to the product ([T26](/specification/theorems/structural), [T29](/specification/theorems/extended)). Density is a scaling property of A4, not an additional claim about matching — which is why it is a lemma rather than an axiom or a theorem.

---

**T1 — The Flywheel Self-Accelerates.** When collective edge *e* > 0, each cycle of the protocol produces more surplus than it consumes: certified edge attracts confidence, confidence funds reinvestment, and reinvestment mints more trust than the cycle started with.

**Derives from** A1+A2+A4+A5+A7. **Consequence:** growth does not require external subsidy — it requires only that collective edge stay positive. This is the mechanism behind the trust flywheel described in [How It Works](/about/mechanism).

---

**T2 — Internal Matching Rate Rises with N.** As membership *N* grows, the fraction of flow matched internally (IMR) rises, because more opposing conviction becomes available to net against within any given cycle.

**Derives from** A4+L1+T1. **Consequence:** growth is self-reinforcing at the mechanical level — more members makes matching more efficient, independent of any single member's skill.

---

**T3 — The Community Becomes the Market.** As IMR approaches its ceiling, virtually all flow matches inside the community, and the community's own aggregate activity becomes indistinguishable from "the market" for internal purposes. What remains outside is merit ($P6$) determining who receives what.

**Derives from** T2+A5. **Consequence:** the distinction between "the platform" and "the market" collapses at scale — the protocol's internal state stops being a subset of the market and becomes a peer to it.

**Proof:** [Proof 4 in the constitution](/specification/proofs/proof-4-community-market).

---

**T4 — retired from the numbering.** T4 does not appear as an independent theorem. Its derivation (A2+A7+A13) and its conclusion are identical to [T11](/specification/theorems/inversion) applied specifically to INDX, so T4 is carried inside T11 rather than stated twice. The theorem count treats T4 as absorbed, not as a gap.
