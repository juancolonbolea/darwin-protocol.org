Spec identity: **~~P7~~ — Inversion.** Demoted as a primitive (spec v7), recognised as a protocol operation (spec v8b).

---

## Definition

Inversion is: state the convention, invert it, test the result for coherence. It is not something the protocol *has* — it is what the protocol *does*, continuously, on every tick. The method that originally produced the spec's axioms is the same method the protocol runs operationally, applied to each of P0's three dimensions:

| Inversion | Dimension | Mechanisms | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Credibility | hidden → certified, public, comparable | A2 (certification) + A8 (standardisation) + A13 (transparency) | Information **created** at the individual level |
| 2 — Intent | exploited → protected, mutualized | A9 (no private extraction) + A14 (power inversion) | Information **protected** at the community level |
| 3 — Data | proprietary → open, commons | P5 (Dataset) + open-dataset theorem T30 | Information **liberated** at the society level |

The three inversions run simultaneously, not sequentially, and each resolves a named market failure: credibility inversion resolves adverse selection, intent inversion resolves moral hazard, data inversion resolves information externality.

## Type

Generative method — explicitly not a stock, flow, or process the way the other six primitives are typed.

## Verb & circle

No verb, no circle. The spec marks both as struck through (~~—~~) precisely because inversion is not located in any one of the three information circles; it is what moves them.

## Irreducibility test — and why it failed as a primitive

Inversion was tested against the same bar as the other six terms and did not clear it, for the opposite reason most primitives fail to clear it. The other primitives are irreducible because they cannot be built from anything else. Inversion is *not a thing at all* in that sense — it is a procedure. A procedure cannot be a primitive in a system where primitives are undefined terms the procedure operates on. That is why it was demoted, not why it was discarded: the demotion is itself the result of applying the inversion test to inversion.

## Grounding

Even demoted, inversion is load-bearing: it is the single mechanism behind every row of the spec's ten institutional inversions (traditional finance's additive intermediation becomes the protocol's subtractive removal, secrecy-creates-value becomes openness-creates-value, and so on). The DARWIN Protocol, read operationally, **is** P7/Inversion applied to each dimension of P0, three times, every cycle. Canonical treatment: `spec/CONSTITUTION.md` §I (row ~~P7~~) and `spec/PROTOCOL.md` §3 (The Three Inversions).
