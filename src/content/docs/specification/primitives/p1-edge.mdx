Spec identity: **P1 — Credibility (e)**

---

## Definition

Credibility is earned by producing edge and certified by the protocol. It accumulates in the Quote — the risk-adjusted, unfalsifiable track record the risk engine attests to on every trade. Credibility cannot be claimed; it can only be built, one attested trade at a time.

## Type

Stock, with a rate: $\dfrac{dC}{dt}$. Credibility is not a single event — it is a level that trades add to over time, and the rate at which it grows is itself a tracked quantity in the trust equation (`spec/PROTOCOL.md` §6).

## Verb & circle

**Develop.** Circle: **e** (the edge circle) — one of the three overlapping domains whose non-overlap area is P0.

## Irreducibility test

Credibility cannot be derived from trust or confidence alone. Both of those require credibility to already exist — trust is the residual of credibility meeting confidence (P3), and confidence is capital allocated in response to observed credibility (P2). What credibility itself requires is something neither trust nor confidence can supply: actual performance against the market. There is no shortcut around producing edge.

## Grounding

P1 is the first resolution in the causal chain: it resolves the **credibility dimension** of P0 (information asymmetry) — the individual-level question "is this merit real?" Resolving it converts human capital into a certified, comparable signal. Everything downstream — confidence, trust, matching, the dataset — depends on credibility existing first. Canonical treatment: `spec/CONSTITUTION.md` §I (row P1), `spec/DEVELOP.md`.
