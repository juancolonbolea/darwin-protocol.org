Spec identity: **P6 — Merit**

---

## Definition

Merit is the ordering principle: **contribution should determine reward.** It is the anti-P0 — where P0 says information *is* asymmetric (a description of the world), P6 says information *should be* symmetric (a value commitment). "Allocate by merit" reduces to "allocate based on symmetric information about quality." Merit is the gravity that pulls the three circles of P0 toward concentricity.

## Type

Ordering principle — and, uniquely among the seven primitives, **normative** rather than descriptive.

## Verb & circle

No dedicated verb. Circle: **Gravity** — not one of the three information circles (e, ★, X), but the force acting on all of them.

## Irreducibility test

Merit cannot be derived from any other primitive. Credibility, confidence, trust, matching, and the dataset are all descriptive — they characterise how selection-driven systems behave once information asymmetry creates pressure. None of them, alone or combined, yields an *ought*. Merit is the ethical foundation the rest of the framework is conditional on, not a conclusion the rest of the framework proves.

## Grounding — value-rootedness

This is the framework's one owned admission: **P6 is a chosen fitness function, not an empirical claim.** The spec states the conditional directly — *if P6, then the theorems.* Without accepting merit as the ordering principle, the descriptive theorems still hold as claims about selection dynamics (the flywheel still self-accelerates, $e^X$ is still algebraic), but they carry no normative force. "The protocol evolves toward merit-determined allocation" is only a *good thing* if P6 is accepted. This is not a hidden weakness — every downstream structure (routing legitimacy, INDX scoring, the 15/85/75 distribution) is conditional on P6 and transparent about being so.

**Transition note.** During transition, capital still determines reward more than contribution does — P6 becomes fully true only once DWX:INDX reaches parity. Before that point, P6 is aspirational, held to the same honest framing as the rest of the spec's transition-state claims. Canonical treatment: `spec/CONSTITUTION.md` §0 (Value-Rootedness) and §I (row P6).
