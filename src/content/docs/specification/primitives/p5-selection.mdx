Spec identity: **P5 — The Dataset**

The site label for this page is "Selection." The spec's own name for P5 is **the Dataset** — a stock of accumulated track records, not a selection mechanism in itself. What follows states the spec's actual definition; where it enables selection dynamics elsewhere in the protocol, that connection is made explicit rather than assumed.

---

## Definition

P5 is the protocol's dataset: 12 years of history, 100,000+ certified track records, matching patterns, and risk-adjusted histories, accumulated one attested trade at a time. It is the vehicle for the P3 → P1 feedback loop — trust flowing back into edge — because a richer dataset makes credibility easier to certify and easier to compare.

The "selection" framing on this site refers to what the dataset *enables*, not to a separate primitive. Because participation runs on fair, uniform terms (A3), the dataset accumulates only from members who chose to keep developing edge under those terms — self-selection is a consequence of open, symmetric terms, not something P5 does directly.

## Type

Stock asset — accumulated, non-portable, protocol-owned.

## Verb & circle

No dedicated verb. Circle: **e × ★** — the bridge between the edge circle and the community circle.

## Irreducibility test

The dataset cannot be derived from credibility or matching alone. Individual credibility (P1) produces one track record; matching (P4) produces one transaction. Neither, by itself, produces the accumulated, cross-referenceable history that lets the protocol compare traders against each other and improve certification over time. The dataset is the compounding of many P1 events across many matching cycles — something no single primitive generates on its own.

## Grounding

P5 resolves the **data dimension** of P0: the society-level question "what information exists?" Where credibility asymmetry is resolved individually and intent asymmetry is resolved communally, data asymmetry is resolved by making the accumulated dataset open inside the protocol — non-rivalrous to members, rivalrous only to outsiders who have no access to it. This is Inversion 3 of the three information inversions (see [P8 — Inversion](/specification/primitives/p8-inversion/)).
