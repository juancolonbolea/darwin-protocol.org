Spec identity: **not one of the spec's seven primitives.** This page is grounded in Axiom A13 (Transparency Inversion) and the data-asymmetry dimension of P0 — not in a canonical primitive of its own. Said plainly: the spec numbers seven primitives, P0 through P6, and P6 there is **Merit** (carried on this site by [P7 — Alignment](/specification/primitives/p7-alignment/)). "Transparency" as this site frames it is a real and load-bearing concept in the spec, but it lives at the axiom layer, not the primitive layer.

---

## What it is, honestly

The spec's transparency claim is precise, and it is narrower than "everything is public." Axiom A13 inverts credibility asymmetry: what used to be hidden — a trader's real, risk-adjusted performance — becomes certified, public, and comparable. That is the transparency the protocol actually delivers: **results are public; the methods that produced them are not.** A Quote is visible to every member. The strategy that generated it is not disclosed, because disclosure isn't what resolves the asymmetry — attested outcomes are.

This is why the concept traces back to P0 rather than forward from a dedicated primitive. P0's data-asymmetry dimension asks a society-level question: "what information exists?" A13 is one lever the protocol pulls to shrink that gap — the credibility-asymmetry lever, specifically, applied continuously. P5 (the Dataset) is the primitive that does the heavier lifting on the data dimension directly.

## Grounding

- **A13 — Transparency Inversion** is the axiom this page is actually about. Combined with A2 (certification) and A8 (standardisation), it is one of three mechanisms behind Inversion 1 in the spec's three-inversions framework — see [P8 — Inversion](/specification/primitives/p8-inversion/).
- **P0's data-asymmetry dimension** is the problem-level statement this page answers to: knowledge locked up, resolved at the society level by P5.

Neither of these is a primitive in its own right on this page's terms — they are, respectively, an axiom and a dimension of a primitive. Canonical treatment: `spec/CONSTITUTION.md` §II (Axioms, A13), `spec/PROTOCOL.md` §2 (P0) and §3 (Inversion 1).
