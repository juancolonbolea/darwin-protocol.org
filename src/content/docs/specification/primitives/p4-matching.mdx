Spec identity: **P4 — Matching**

---

## Definition

Matching is the verb the protocol performs across five dimensions: flow (buy and sell orders across members), knowledge (experienced traders with learners), capital (investors with skilled traders), community (mentors, educators, tool builders), and tool (analytics, diagnostics, datasets). It is caused by developing credibility and honouring confidence, and its effect is that opposing flows find each other inside the protocol instead of leaking out to external, extraction-charging venues.

The spec is explicit that matching is a **welcome by-product**, not the goal. The goal is resolving information asymmetry; matching is what that resolution looks like in practice once enough members are developing edge and allocating capital.

## Type

Process (by-product), not a stock or a flow in the accounting sense — it is an activity the protocol continuously performs rather than a quantity that accumulates.

## Verb & circle

No dedicated verb (matching is itself the verb). Circle: **★** (the community circle).

## Irreducibility test

Matching cannot be reduced to credibility or confidence alone. It requires both already in motion — members developing P1 and capital honouring P2 — before there is anything to match. Its irreducibility comes from being the emergent behaviour of the other two primitives operating together at scale, not a separate mechanism bolted on top.

## Grounding

Matching resolves the **intent dimension** of P0: the community-level question "what are others doing with my flow?" Left unresolved, intent asymmetry is what lets counterparties exploit visible order flow. The protocol's answer is mutualized, internal matching — a sole counterparty structure that hides individual intent while still executing it. This is Inversion 2 of the three information inversions (see [P8 — Inversion](/specification/primitives/p8-inversion/)).
