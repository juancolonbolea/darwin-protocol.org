## Members are defined by what they do

In the DARWIN Economy, your role is not a title. It is a verb. Every member contributes through one or more of five actions — and every action feeds the same engine: INDX minting.

You are not assigned a role. You choose your actions. Most members specialise. All earn INDX proportional to the value they create.

---

## The five member actions

### 1. Source

**Bring new edge into the economy.**

You are the scouts. You recruit talented traders, find untapped markets, open new geographies. Every skilled trader you bring in adds to the dataset, improves the selection, and strengthens the community.

When a trader you sourced builds a successful DARWIN, you earn from the edge you introduced. The protocol tracks the provenance.

### 2. Develop

**Coach, teach, build tools. Make other members better.**

Your income is measured by your students' results — not your opinion of their progress, but their verified Quote improvement. The protocol certifies the development. You coach. The math verifies.

Educators, mentors, and tool builders fall here. The marketplace displays your track record as a developer of talent — verified, public, unfalsifiable.

### 3. Contribute

**Trade. Submit signals. Build your track record.**

This is the raw edge the economy runs on. Every trade you make feeds the dataset. Every signal you contribute improves the community's collective intelligence. Your DARWIN is your contribution — attested, scored, and available for investment.

### 4. Process

**Model the data. Analyse the 100K dataset. Build allocation strategies.**

You turn raw edge into investable signals. Signal processors work with 12+ years of verified track records — winners and losers — to identify patterns, build portfolios, and improve the community's selection algorithms.

### 5. Compound

**Deploy capital. Back the winners. Make the trust stock bigger.**

Capital compounders invest in the DARWINs the community has identified as skilled. Every euro deployed grows the trust stock. When the trust stock grows, everyone's earning potential grows.

## Everyone can do everything

There is no hierarchy. A trader who contributes edge can also process data and compound capital. An educator who develops talent can also source new members. The protocol measures contribution across all five dimensions and rewards accordingly.

The only rule: **contribution must be measurable**. The risk engine, the dataset, and the marketplace produce the measurements. No self-reporting. No subjective assessment. The math decides.

---

## One community

Thirteen thousand traders from over 70 countries. Signal providers, signal processors, educators, capital compounders, and scouts — all operating under the same rules, measured by the same engine, earning from the same trust stock.

When INDX beats the market, every contributor wins. Your individual action feeds the collective proof. The collective proof attracts capital. Capital rewards your action.

This is what "Develop And Reward With INdX" looks like in practice: a community where everyone's contribution compounds into everyone else's opportunity.
