## Prove it and get paid

The DARWIN Economy offers two types of challenges: **DarwinIA** (included with every membership) and **Boosters** (optional, for those who want to stake on themselves). Both allocate real capital to real performance.

---

## DarwinIA — included with membership

DarwinIA is a monthly capital allocation competition. No entry fee. Your DARWIN score qualifies you. Pure merit.

### DarwinIA Silver

- **Capital allocated:** EUR 30,000 to EUR 200,000
- **Duration:** 3 months
- **Qualification:** By DARWIN rating
- **Cost:** Included with membership

Qualify through your rating. Receive a capital allocation. Trade it. Earn 15% of gains generated on the allocated capital. No risk to you beyond your normal trading.

### DarwinIA Gold

- **Capital allocated:** Up to EUR 500,000
- **Duration:** 6 months
- **Qualification:** By rank on the leaderboard
- **Cost:** Included with membership

The top tier. Reserved for traders who have demonstrated sustained, high-quality performance. Larger allocations, longer evaluation periods, greater earning potential.

### How qualification works

There is no application process. There is no committee. The risk engine produces your DARWIN score. Your score determines your eligibility. If you qualify, you are in. If you do not, trade better.

## Boosters — stake on yourself

Boosters are optional challenges for traders who want amplified capital allocation. You pay a premium. You receive a capital allocation. You beat the benchmark. You earn 15% of the gains.

| Tier | Premium | Capital Allocated |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **Starter** | EUR 125 | EUR 25,000 |
| **Standard** | EUR 250 | EUR 50,000 |
| **Pro** | EUR 465 | EUR 100,000 |
| **Elite** | EUR 930 | EUR 200,000 |

### Stacking

You can stack multiple Boosters simultaneously, up to EUR 400,000 in total allocated capital. This means a trader confident in their edge can scale their exposure progressively.

### The economics

You pay the premium upfront. If you beat the benchmark over the evaluation period, you earn 15% of the gains on the allocated capital. If you do not beat the benchmark, you lose the premium. That is the bet.

Boosters are designed for traders who have enough confidence in their track record to put money behind it. The economics only make sense if you genuinely believe in your edge — which is exactly the filter they are designed to be.

## The permanent challenge

Beyond DarwinIA and Boosters, there is a challenge that never ends.

**INDX vs. the market.**

INDX is the community's collective performance — a composite of the best DARWINs, selected by the protocol. Every day, INDX is measured against the broader market.

The claim is simple: *a community of independent traders, selecting each other through transparent merit, produces better risk-adjusted returns than the opaque institutional market.*

INDX beating the benchmark is the proof. Every member who contributes edge, processes data, deploys capital, or develops other traders is contributing to this permanent challenge.

When INDX outperforms, it is not a marketing statistic. It is the entire community's collective answer to a question the financial industry has been asking for decades: can independents compete?

The answer, verified daily by the risk engine, is yes.
