## Three stages. One escalator.

The DARWIN Economy has three stages. Everyone enters the same way. How far you go depends entirely on what you contribute.

---

## PLAY (User)

**EUR 45/month. You are building.**

You trade. You learn. You build your track record. You use the marketplace — coaches, tools, analytics. You enter DarwinIA challenges. You access the 100K dataset and its diagnostics.

At this stage, you are a net payer. That is by design. The membership fee is high enough that tourists leave. Low enough that no one with genuine ambition is gated.

But you are building the most valuable thing in the DARWIN Economy: **a verified, public track record that no one can take from you.** Every trade logged. Every position scored. Every day, your DARWIN grows.

The PLAY stage is not a waiting room. It is a training ground with real tools, real data, and real consequences.

---

## EARN (Member)

**The meritocratic gate. First INDX minted or earned.**

The moment you mint your first INDX, you cross the gate. You are now a net earner. The protocol recognizes your contribution.

You can earn through any of the five member actions:

- **Source** — Bring talented traders into the economy. Earn from the edge you introduced.
- **Develop** — Coach and teach. Earn from your students' verified improvement.
- **Contribute** — Trade and submit signals. Earn royalties from investors who back your DARWIN.
- **Process** — Analyse the dataset. Build allocation strategies. Earn from the investable signals you create.
- **Compound** — Deploy capital. Back the winners. Earn returns on your investment.

Most members earn through one or two actions. Some earn through all five. The protocol measures contribution across every dimension and rewards proportionally.

**This is the gate that matters.** When your INDX earned exceeds your membership cost, you have proven something: you create more value than you consume. Everything after that compounds.

---

## OWN (Owner)

**Your money compounds alongside the community's best.**

You have accumulated enough INDX to convert to DWX — ownership in the protocol itself. Your capital, your earnings, and your reputation all compound together.

As an Owner, you benefit from every improvement in the economy. When new members join and contribute edge. When the dataset grows and selection improves. When capital flows in and the trust stock expands. Your ownership appreciates because the community you helped build is getting better.

DWX is not purchased. It is earned — through INDX, which is earned through contribution. The ownership pathway is meritocratic from start to finish.

**Permanently aligned.** The better everyone does, the better you do. There is no way to profit from the community's failure. The architecture makes extraction impossible.

---

## The escalator

| Stage | Status | Economics | What you are building |
|-------|--------|-----------|----------------------|
| **PLAY** | User | Net payer (EUR 45/month) | Track record, skills, reputation |
| **EARN** | Member | Net earner (INDX > cost) | Income streams, community value |
| **OWN** | Owner | Capital compounder (DWX) | Protocol ownership, legacy |

The transition from PLAY to EARN is the critical moment. It is the meritocratic gate — the point where the economy recognizes that you deserve a seat at the table.

There is no committee that decides when you cross. There is no application. The math decides. When your contribution generates INDX, you are a Member.

## Case Study: Maria, the Educator

Maria is a former institutional trader who pivoted to teaching. She joins the DARWIN Economy as a Catalyst.

### Month 1-3 (PLAY)

Maria pays EUR 45/month. She accesses the 100K dataset — 12 years of verified track records, winners AND losers. She uses the diagnostic tools to build a training curriculum. She identifies the 7 most common failure patterns in the dataset. She publishes her coaching profile on the marketplace.

At this stage, Maria has not earned a single euro from the economy. But she has something no independent trading educator has ever had before: access to a verified, comprehensive dataset of real trading outcomes. Not hypotheticals. Not backtests. Real traders, real money, real results — over 12 years.

### Month 4-12 (Developing)

Her first 12 students join. Each gets a DARWIN — their public, risk-adjusted track record. Maria coaches them using the dataset:

*"You have the same drawdown pattern as 4,000 other traders in the archive. Here is what the ones who survived did differently."*

The protocol **certifies student development**. Not Maria's opinion — the Quote. Each student's DARWIN improvement is measured by the risk engine. Maria's coaching impact is tracked:

- Average student Quote improvement
- Retention rate
- Time-to-profitability
- Student survival rate vs. dataset average

The marketplace displays her track record as an educator — verified, public, unfalsifiable. Just like a DARWIN for trading, but for teaching. Investors and prospective students can see exactly how effective her coaching is, measured by the same engine that measures trading skill.

### Month 12+ (EARN)

Maria earns INDX proportional to her students' DARWIN improvements. The economics:

- When her students' Quotes go up, her income goes up.
- When her students enter DarwinIA and win capital allocations, she earns a share.
- When investors back her students' DARWINs, she earns from the ecosystem she built.

She brings in 3 more educators from her network (Source action). Each one's students add to the dataset. The dataset gets richer. Everyone's diagnostics improve. Maria's original 12 students benefit from data generated by the new cohorts.

Her income is now diversified across multiple streams, all tied to one thing: the verified improvement of the people she teaches.

### Month 24+ (OWN)

Maria has earned enough INDX to deploy as capital inside the community. She is now an Owner. Her coaching business, her INDX earnings, and her capital returns all compound together. The better the community does — including the 50+ traders she personally developed — the better she does.

### What Maria never did

- She never held anyone's money.
- She never issued a "certificate" herself.
- She never asked anyone to "trust her."

The **protocol** certified her students' development through the Quote. She coached. The math verified. Investors trusted the verification. Capital followed.

### What this means

This is what **Develop And Reward With INdX** means for an educator: your students' verified improvement IS your income. No invoices. No "trust me." No subjective testimonials. The protocol certifies it.

Maria's story is not hypothetical. It is the design target. The DARWIN Economy is built so that anyone who genuinely develops other members' edge — through coaching, tools, analytics, or mentorship — earns proportionally to the value they create. Measured by the same engine. Verified by the same protocol. Trusted by the same market.
