## Real markets. Real money. One leaderboard.

The DARWIN Economy is a massively multiplayer online game about trading. Thirteen thousand players from over 70 countries. Real markets. Real money. Real consequences. One risk engine. One leaderboard. Same rules for everyone.

No pay-to-win. No shortcuts. The only way up is through verified performance.

---

## How to play

### Sign up
Thirty seconds. OAuth. You are in.

### Verify your identity
Real people, real track records. The economy does not accept anonymous participants. Your DARWIN is attached to a verified identity — this is what makes the track records credible.

### Start trading
Virtual or live, same conditions. The risk engine scores both. Virtual trading lets you build a track record before committing real capital. When you are ready, the transition is seamless — your history follows you.

### Get scored
Every trade produces a data point. The risk engine normalises your performance to a standard risk scale (6.5% monthly VaR). The result is your **DARWIN** — your public, risk-adjusted, unfalsifiable track record.

Nobody can inflate it. Nobody can take it away. It is built one trade at a time.

### Climb the leaderboard
Your DARWIN is ranked against every other trader in the economy. The leaderboard is the meritocracy made visible. Rank determines capital allocation in DarwinIA challenges. Rank determines investor attention. Rank determines your earning potential.

## The risk engine: the referee

The risk engine is what makes this a fair game. It normalises every trader to the same risk scale, so skill — not leverage — determines the ranking.

A trader running conservative 2% risk and a trader running aggressive 20% risk are scored on identical terms. The engine strips out the noise and measures the signal: can this person generate risk-adjusted returns?

The risk engine is independent. You cannot influence it, negotiate with it, or appeal to it. It measures. It publishes. That is all.

## The compound advantage

Here is what makes the DARWIN Economy different from trading alone: the community's collective intelligence gives every member an edge.

**The 100K dataset** — Twelve years of verified track records. Winners and losers. Every drawdown, every recovery, every failure pattern, every success signal. A trader joining today benefits from data accumulated since the economy's founding.

**Diagnostics** — The dataset powers tools that help you understand your trading. Where do your drawdowns happen? How does your strategy compare to the 4,000 others with similar characteristics? What did the survivors do differently?

**Peer benchmarks** — You are not trading in isolation. You can see how you compare to every other trader in the economy, normalised to the same risk scale.

And the trader joining tomorrow benefits from your data. Every trade you make enriches the dataset for everyone.

## Why "game"?

Because the best games have three properties:

1. **Clear rules** — everyone knows how scoring works
2. **Fair competition** — the same rules apply to everyone
3. **Meaningful stakes** — performance has real consequences

The DARWIN Economy has all three. You enter knowing exactly how you will be scored. You compete on identical terms with every other trader in the world. And your performance determines real capital allocation, real earnings, and a real reputation.

The difference from other games: this one pays you for being good at it. And the prize pool grows every time a new skilled player joins.
