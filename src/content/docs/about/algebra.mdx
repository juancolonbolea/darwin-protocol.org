## Two variables that determine everything

The DARWIN Economy runs on a simple equation. Two variables. One outcome.

**$e$** = your edge. Risk-adjusted performance. Your skill, measured by the risk engine.

**$X$** = the trust stock. The community's accumulated capital and credibility.

---

## Your earnings

$$\text{Your earnings} = e \times X$$

Your edge, applied to the trust stock.

This means two things grow your income:

1. **Improve your edge** — trade better, and your share of the trust stock grows.
2. **The community grows** — when more capital enters the economy, everyone's earnings grow.

You are not competing against other members for a fixed pie. The pie grows when any member contributes. Your edge determines your slice.

---

## The community's growth

The collective growth of the DARWIN Economy is not linear. It is exponential:

$$\text{Community growth} = e^X$$

Why exponential? Because contributions compound. When a trader improves, the dataset improves. When the dataset improves, other traders improve. When investors allocate capital, the trust stock grows. When the trust stock grows, more traders are attracted. Each input amplifies the others.

This is the fundamental difference between the DARWIN Economy and a traditional fund. In a fund, growth is additive — one more investor adds one more unit of capital. In the DARWIN Economy, growth is multiplicative — one more skilled trader improves the data, the selection, the capital attraction, and the infrastructure for everyone.

## INDX: the proof

**INDX** is the community's collective performance metric. It represents the risk-adjusted returns of the best DARWINs, selected by the protocol.

When INDX beats the market, a specific claim is proven: this community of independent traders, selecting each other through transparent merit, produces better risk-adjusted returns than the opaque institutional market.

INDX is not a marketing number. It is the scoreboard. When it goes up, trust is being minted. When trust is minted, the economy grows. When the economy grows, your earnings grow.
