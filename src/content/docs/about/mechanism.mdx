## The simple version

You join. You trade. Every trade gets logged by a risk engine that you cannot influence, bribe, or fool. The engine produces a **Quote** — your public, risk-adjusted, unfalsifiable track record. Capital follows the Quote.

That is the entire system in four sentences.

---

## Step by step

### 1. You trade

Connect to any broker running DARWIN rails. Trade the way you normally would — your strategy, your markets, your timing. The protocol does not tell you how to trade. It measures the outcome.

### 2. Every trade gets scored

The risk engine logs every position. It normalises your performance to a standard risk scale (6.5% monthly VaR). This means a cautious trader running 2% risk and an aggressive trader running 20% risk are compared on identical terms. Skill, not leverage.

### 3. Your Quote is published

Your DARWIN Quote is your reputation. It is:

- **Attested** — produced by the risk engine, not by you
- **Unfalsifiable** — you cannot edit, delete, or alter a single trade
- **Public** — visible to every member of the economy
- **Built one trade at a time** — there are no shortcuts

The Quote is the universal currency of merit in the DARWIN Economy. It answers one question: can this person trade?

### 4. Capital follows skill

Your DARWIN is listed on the exchange. Investors — from individual members deploying their own capital to institutional allocators — can back your track record. When they do, you earn a share of the performance they receive.

### 5. You earn

When investors profit from your DARWIN, you earn 15% of their gains. No invoices. No sales calls. The protocol handles everything: risk management, settlement, compliance, and distribution.

## The matching engine

Behind the scenes, the protocol runs a matching engine that pools all trading flow within the community. When one member buys EUR/USD and another sells it, the trades are matched internally — no need to go to the external market. This is called **internal matching**.

Internal matching reduces costs for everyone. The higher the internal matching rate, the lower the transaction costs, the better the execution, the more surplus generated. That surplus is reinvested into the community.

The matching engine operates across five dimensions:

| Dimension | What it matches | Surplus generated |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Flow** | Buy and sell orders across members | Lower transaction costs |
| **Knowledge** | Experienced traders with learners | Faster skill development |
| **Capital** | Investors with skilled traders | Better allocation |
| **Community** | Mentors, educators, tool builders | Shared infrastructure |
| **Tool** | Analytics, diagnostics, datasets | Better decision-making |

Each match rescues surplus that would otherwise be lost to external friction. That surplus is reinvested, compounding the community's advantage.
